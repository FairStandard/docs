Assigning a module hands a client a fixed lesson list to work through, separate from the open catalog they can also browse on their own. This walks you through <span className="kw">picking</span> the module, the client, and, if you want one, the case.

## Before you begin

You need a client in Vero to assign to. A case isn't required — an assignment can stand on its own, for general education before a case exists or alongside one.

1. From the sidebar, select **Learning Center**.
2. Select **Browse modules** and open the one you want to assign.
3. Select **Assign**.
4. Choose the client.
5. If this assignment belongs to a case, select it; otherwise leave it unattached.
6. Confirm the assignment.

## What happens next

The version of the module you were looking at when you assigned it is the version pinned to that assignment. If a newer version of the same module is published later, the client's lesson list doesn't move to it — you'd assign the newer version separately if you want them to see it. The assignment now shows up in your workspace's **Assigned modules** list, alongside every other assignment your firm has made, not narrowed to just this one.

<Note>
  Vero explains legal process. It does not give legal advice.
</Note>
