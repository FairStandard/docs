Progress is tracked per lesson, under the specific assignment you made — not against the module in general. This walks you through finding one client's assignment and reading their <span className="kw">progress</span> on it.

## Before you begin

You need an assignment already made for this client. If nothing has been assigned to them yet, assign a module first.

1. From the sidebar, select **Learning Center**.
2. Select **Assigned modules**.
3. Find the client's assignment in the list — each row shows the module, the pinned version, and the case it's tied to, if any.
4. Open the assignment.
5. Select **Progress**.

## What happens next

The progress list shows each lesson the client has marked complete, in the order the module lists them. An empty list means they haven't started yet, not that anything is wrong. A lesson that's been marked complete more than once doesn't show up twice — it's recorded once regardless of how many times it was submitted.

The list you're checking isn't filtered to assignments you personally made — if you have more than one attorney on a case or in your workspace, you're seeing every assignment on record, not only your own. Check the assignment's own details to confirm who assigned it and when.
