A module assigned to your case shows up as something to work through, not just something in the open catalog. This walks you through reading it and <span className="kw">tracking</span> your progress.

## Before you begin

You need a module assigned to your case. If nothing has been assigned yet, browse the open catalog instead — it has the same content, without tracked progress.

1. From your case, select **Learning Center**.
2. Select **Assigned to you**.
3. Choose the module you want to work on.
4. Read the lessons in order, starting from the top.
5. At the end of each lesson, select **Mark complete**.
6. Check **Your progress** on the module to see which lessons you've finished.

## What happens next

The lesson list you're reading stays exactly as it was the day the module was assigned to you, even if a newer version of the same module is published later. That means your progress never gets orphaned partway through by content changing underneath you.

Marking a lesson complete more than once doesn't create a duplicate or an error — it stays marked once. If you close a module partway through, your completed lessons are still marked when you come back.
