The full module catalog is open to browse any time, whether or not anything has been assigned to you. This walks you through <span className="kw">filtering</span> it down to what applies to your case.

## Before you begin

You don't need anything assigned to you to browse the catalog — every current module is visible to anyone with a case.

1. From your case, select **Learning Center**.
2. Select **Browse modules**.
3. Filter by practice area, if the list is showing more than one.
4. Filter by state, if you want modules written specifically for your jurisdiction rather than a general one.
5. Select a module to open it.

## What happens next

The module opens to its first lesson, with the rest listed in order below it. If a lesson cites a specific law, the text shown is the current version of that citation, resolved when you open the lesson rather than fixed at the time the module was written.

Reading a module from the catalog doesn't create a progress record for you — that only happens for modules assigned to your case. If you want your progress tracked, check whether the module you're reading is also listed under your assigned modules; if it isn't, there's nothing to mark complete, just content to read.

<Note>
  Vero explains legal process. It does not give legal advice.
</Note>
