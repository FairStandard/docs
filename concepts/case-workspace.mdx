Your case lives in one place. The Vero case workspace gives you — and your attorney, if you have one — a single, shared view of everything that matters: uploaded evidence, assembled law, procedural deadlines, and direct messaging. The cycle of email check-ins, missed attachments, and conflicting version histories ends here. When something changes, everyone sees it immediately.

## Evidence vault

Every document, recording, and financial record you upload lands in the Evidence Vault. The engine automatically organizes each item, applies category tags, links it to relevant timeline events, and flags it against the applicable law for your case. Nothing sits in an unlabeled folder. When you need to find a specific bank statement or a recording from a particular date, it is already sorted and ready.

## Assembled law

The Assembled Law panel shows the statutes, rule sets, and controlling case law that the Vero engine has identified for your specific case and jurisdiction. You do not have to search for the law yourself — when you created your case, the engine pulled every applicable provision automatically. Browse by code or rule set, or follow citations directly from your evidence and draft filings back to the governing statute.

## Timeline & deadlines

The Timeline gives you a chronological view of your case: key events drawn from your uploaded documents, court orders, and communications sit alongside upcoming procedural deadlines. Deadlines are calculated from your jurisdiction's rules and updated as the case progresses. You can see at a glance what has happened and what is due next.

## Next steps

Next Steps translates the current state of your case into concrete recommended actions. These are not generic reminders — they reflect your actual case status, outstanding filings, approaching deadlines, and gaps identified in your evidence. Work through the list to stay ahead of the case rather than reacting to it.

## Messaging

The Messaging channel inside the workspace keeps all case-related communication in one thread, tied directly to the case record. Attorneys and litigants exchange messages here rather than across scattered email chains. Because the conversation lives inside the workspace, context from evidence and deadlines is always one click away.

<Note>
  Both the litigant and the attorney see the same case workspace. There is no
  separate "client view" — transparency is built in.
</Note>
