Vero is built for everyone who touches a legal case — the person fighting for custody of their child, the attorney managing a docket of forty clients, the mediator brought in to facilitate a settlement, and everyone in between. Your role shapes how the platform presents itself to you and how your workflows are structured. It does not determine what you have access to. The same underlying capabilities are available across the board.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Self-Represented Litigant">
    You are representing yourself — no attorney on file. Vero gives you full access to every feature on the platform. Nothing is locked out or restricted on the assumption that you need an attorney to use it responsibly.

    **What you can do:**
    - Upload all of your evidence and let the engine organize and analyze it
    - Run the Law Assembly Engine on your case type and jurisdiction to see every applicable statute, rule, and precedent
    - Track deadlines and procedural requirements for your court
    - Work through Next Steps to understand what actions your case currently requires
    - Draft and organize filings using the evidence and law the engine has surfaced
    - Access the Learning Center to build your understanding of the process as you go
    - Browse the Marketplace to find attorneys or legal service providers if your needs change

    You have the same tools a represented litigant has. Use all of them.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Represented Litigant">
    You have an attorney on your case. Your attorney is connected to your case workspace and can see everything you see. You still have full access to every platform feature — being represented does not reduce your visibility into your own case.

    **What you can do:**
    - Everything a self-represented litigant can do
    - Communicate with your attorney directly inside the case workspace, with full context from evidence and deadlines always available
    - Do your own organizing, tagging, and uploading to reduce the time your attorney spends on intake — time they would otherwise bill you for
    - Review the assembled law and evidence analysis so you understand what your attorney is working with, not just what they choose to tell you
    - Track deadlines yourself rather than depending on a phone call to know what is coming

    You are not a passive participant in your case. Vero keeps you as informed and involved as you choose to be.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Attorney">
    You manage one or more client cases on Vero. Each case gives you a shared workspace with your client — you both see the same evidence vault, assembled law, timeline, and messaging thread.

    **What you can do:**
    - Access the full Law Assembly Engine for every case you manage, with complete statutory and precedent coverage for that jurisdiction and case type
    - Review and build on evidence analysis produced by the engine — patterns, violations, and discrepancies are surfaced and cited before you open a file
    - Manage deadlines and procedural requirements across your entire docket from a single interface
    - Communicate with clients inside the case workspace rather than across scattered email threads
    - List your practice in the Marketplace so prospective clients in your jurisdiction and practice area can find and retain you directly through the platform
    - Accept new client cases through the platform without a separate intake process

    Vero compresses the time between case intake and trial-ready preparation. The engine does the assembly and organization work; you apply judgment to what it surfaces.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Legal Service Provider">
    You are a notary, custody evaluator, mediator, forensic accountant, or other professional who supports legal proceedings without representing a party. Vero makes you discoverable to litigants and attorneys who need your services and gives you a structured workflow for managing the consultations and deliverables that follow.

    **What you can do:**
    - List your credentials, service area, and availability in the Marketplace
    - Receive referrals and consultation requests directly through the platform
    - Access a focused consultation workflow scoped to the engagement — you see what is relevant to your role, not the entire case record
    - Deliver findings and reports through the platform so they land directly in the requesting party's Evidence Vault, already tied to the case

    Your Marketplace listing is your practice profile on Vero. Keep it current and it works as a passive referral channel.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Your role affects how Vero presents the product — not what you can
  do. Every role has access to the same underlying capabilities.
</Note>

## Setting your role

You select your role when you create your Vero account. The onboarding flow asks for your state, your role, and — if you are a litigant — your case type. These selections trigger the initial law assembly and configure your workspace.

If your situation changes — for example, you were self-represented and have now retained an attorney, or you are an attorney adding a provider credential — contact Vero support to update your role. Some role changes can be made from your account settings directly.
