Most of your billable time is already recorded before you sit down to review it — case-linked work you do inside Vero accrues on its own. This walks you through checking that time, locking it in, and turning it into a <span className="kw">paid</span> invoice.

1. From the sidebar, select **Admin**, then **Time**.
2. Review the entries already captured automatically. Add a manual entry for anything that happened outside Vero, and mark each entry billable or not.
3. Select **Submit** on the entries you want counted this billing cycle.
4. Approve the submitted entries, then lock them. Only an approved or locked entry can be pulled onto an invoice.
5. From the sidebar, select **Admin**, then **Invoicing**. Select **New invoice**, and choose the client and case.
6. Pull in the locked time entries you want billed, and add any manual lines you need.
7. Select **Send**.
8. When the client pays, mark the invoice paid: draw the amount from their retainer account, or record it as paid outside Vero if they paid you another way.

## Before you begin

You need an approved or locked time entry before you can put it on an invoice — a submitted entry that has not been approved is not eligible yet. If you have not opened a retainer account for this client's case and plan to draw the payment from it, set that up first.

## What happens next

Once locked, a time entry is never edited again. If you find a mistake in one afterward, add a correcting entry instead — it carries its own record, and the original stays exactly as it was. An invoice that has been sent can still be voided if it should not have gone out; it cannot be deleted.

Marking an invoice paid does not charge anyone's card. Drawing from a retainer moves money that was already funded on the account; recording it as paid outside Vero is a note that the amount was settled some other way. Neither one connects to your firm's outside accounting software — that connection is a named capability with nothing built behind it yet, so the entry still needs to go into your own books by hand.
