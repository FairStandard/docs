Most questions here come down to something that has not been built yet rather than something set up wrong, or a step done in the wrong order. The list below covers what firms run into <span className="kw">most</span>.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't find a list of every client across my firm">
    There is no combined client directory today. You work from an individual case or your organization's roster, not one screen listing every client at once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The person I'm adding says they never got an invitation">
    Vero does not send an email invitation to someone who has not signed up. The person needs an existing Vero account before you can add them to your organization or a case — ask them to create one first, then add them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I added someone to my organization but they can't see a specific client's case">
    Organization membership and case membership are separate. Adding someone to your firm does not put them on any case; add them to the specific case as well.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I removed someone by mistake">
    Removal takes effect immediately and cannot be undone from that screen. Add the person back the same way you added them the first time — the effect is the same, even though the earlier removal stays on record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A time entry won't go onto an invoice">
    Only an entry that has been approved or locked is eligible. A submitted entry that has not been approved yet is not billable until it clears that step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I found a mistake in a locked time entry">
    A locked entry is never edited. Add a correcting entry instead — a negative amount to remove time, a positive one to add it back. The original entry and the correction both stay on record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A retainer draw was refused">
    A draw can only take out what the account actually holds — the balance is always the sum of every fund and draw on record, never a stored number. Fund the account further before drawing again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't find a way to update my profile or security settings">
    That self-service page is not built yet beyond signing in and out. There is no in-app way to change those settings today.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a way to connect my accounting software">
    Accounting sync is a named capability with nothing built behind it yet. Billing activity in Vero still needs to be entered into your outside books by hand.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck

If none of the above accounts for what you're seeing, contact [support@fairstandard.com](mailto:support@fairstandard.com) with your organization name and a description of what you expected to see.
