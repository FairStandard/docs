Setting up the firm side of Vero takes a handful of steps, and most firms only do the first two once. This guide walks you through <span className="kw">building</span> your organization, adding the people on it, and taking a piece of work all the way from billable time to a paid invoice.

## What you'll learn

- How to add a colleague, staff member, or client to your organization or a case
- How to open and fund a retainer account
- How to record billable time and move it through submit, approve, and lock
- How to build, send, and pay a client invoice

## Before you begin

You need an owner or attorney role in your organization to add people, manage retainer accounts, or issue invoices. Everyone you add — a colleague, staff member, or client — needs an existing Vero account first; there is no email invitation for someone who has not signed up yet.

### Step 1: Add someone to your organization or a case

From the sidebar, select **Clients**, then select **Invitations**. Choose whether you are adding this person to your organization — as a colleague or staff member — or to one specific case, as an attorney, staff member, or the client themselves. They are added immediately once you confirm; there is nothing further for them to accept.

### Step 2: Open a retainer account

From the sidebar, select **Clients**, then select the client's case. Open a retainer account for it. An account with no funds in it is still a valid account — opening one does not require funding it on the spot.

### Step 3: Fund the retainer

Add a fund entry to the account with the amount and where it came from. The balance on the account is always the sum of every fund and draw recorded against it, so it updates the moment you add one.

### Step 4: Review your captured time

From the sidebar, select **Admin**, then **Time**. Case-linked work you do inside Vero is captured automatically, so most entries for a normal week are already there. Add a manual entry for anything that happened outside Vero, and mark each entry billable or not.

### Step 5: Submit, approve, and lock the time you want billed

Select **Submit** on the entries for this billing cycle, then approve and lock them. Only an approved or locked entry can go onto an invoice — this step is a check, not a formality.

### Step 6: Build and send an invoice

From the sidebar, select **Admin**, then **Invoicing**. Select **New invoice**, choose the client and case, and pull in the locked time entries you want billed. Add any manual lines you need, then select **Send**.

### Step 7: Record payment

When the client pays, mark the invoice paid. Draw the amount from their retainer account, or record it as paid outside Vero if they paid you another way — neither option charges a card on your behalf.

## Tips

- Adding someone to your organization and adding them to a case are separate steps, because they grant different things. Do both if the person needs both.
- A locked time entry is never edited. If you find a mistake, add a correcting entry instead — it carries its own record, and the original stays exactly as it was.
- An invoice does not have to be built entirely from captured time. Add a manual line for anything that was not captured automatically.
