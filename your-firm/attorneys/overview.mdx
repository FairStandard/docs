Running your firm inside Vero is <span className="kw">separate</span> from working a case: it covers who is on your team, who is on each client's matter, what a client owes, and what you personally get paid for. This page covers what the Clients and Admin screens do today, and what still happens outside Vero because it has not been built yet.

## How it works

Your organization is the account your firm operates under — whether that is a solo practice or a multi-attorney firm. Owners and attorneys can add a colleague or staff member to the organization, and separately assign anyone to a specific case as an attorney, staff member, or the case's client. Both of those take effect right away: there is no pending state to wait on, and no separate acceptance step for the person being added. Adding someone to your organization and adding them to a case are two different actions, because the two things they grant are different — organization membership is about your firm as a business, and a case assignment is about one matter.

Retainer accounting works as a real trust ledger: you open a retainer account for a client's case, fund it, and draw against it — usually to pay an invoice. The balance is never a number someone typed in; it is always the sum of every fund and draw on record, so it can be reconstructed and checked at any point.

Billable time capture is mostly automatic. Case-linked work you do inside Vero — drafting, filing, messaging — accrues time to that case on its own, and a manual timer covers anything that happens outside the flow Vero already tracks. Recorded time moves through a review: submitted, then approved, then locked. Only approved or locked time can be pulled onto an invoice, which keeps a rushed entry from turning into a bill before anyone has checked it.

Invoicing builds a bill from that locked time, or from any line you add by hand, and sends it to the client. Getting paid works two ways today: draw the amount from the client's retainer, or record that they paid you outside Vero. Neither one charges a card for you — recording a payment and charging one are different things, and only the first is built.

## What you can do here

- Add a colleague or staff member to your organization, and remove one later.
- Assign an attorney, staff member, or client to a specific case.
- Open a retainer account for a client's case, fund it, and see its balance and full history.
- Record billable time — most of it automatically — and take it through submit, approve, and lock.
- Correct a locked time entry by adding an offsetting entry, rather than editing the original.
- Build an invoice from locked time or manual lines, send it, and mark it paid from retainer or as paid outside Vero.

## Availability

A few things named here are not built yet, and it is worth knowing which before you go looking for them. There is no single screen that lists every client across your organization today — you work from the case or the organization roster, not a combined directory. Adding someone to your organization or a case requires that they already hold a Vero account; sending an email invitation to someone who has not signed up is not built. There is no self-service page yet for updating your own profile or security settings beyond signing in and out. Connecting your firm's outside accounting software is a named capability with no working connection behind it today — your books still need the entry made by hand on your end.
