Adding someone to your firm and adding them to a case are two different actions, because they grant two different things. This walks you through <span className="kw">both</span>.

## Before you begin

The person you are adding needs an existing Vero account. Vero does not yet send an email invitation to someone who has not signed up — if they do not have an account, ask them to create one first.

1. From the sidebar, select **Clients**, then select **Invitations**.
2. Select **Add**.
3. Choose whether you are adding this person to your organization or to one specific case.
4. If it is your organization, choose the account and the role you are giving them: attorney or staff.
5. If it is a case, choose the account and the case relationship they will hold — attorney, staff, or the client themselves.
6. Confirm.

## What happens next

The person is added right away. There is no pending state and no acceptance step for them to complete — the moment you confirm, an organization member can sign in to your firm's workspace at the role you gave them, and a case member shows up on that case's team.

Adding someone to your organization does not put them on any specific case, and adding them to a case does not add them to your organization's roster. If a new attorney at your firm needs both, add them twice.

To remove someone, open the same screen and select **Remove** next to their name. Removal is immediate and does not require their confirmation. If you remove someone by mistake, add them back the same way you added them the first time — nothing about the earlier removal is undone, but the effect is the same.
