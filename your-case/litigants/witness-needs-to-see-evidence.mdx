Your <span className="kw">witness</span> is testifying next week, and they need to see one specific recording before they take the stand — but they have no reason to see the rest of your case, and you'd rather they didn't.

## What to do

Open the record on your Case Detail page and select **Share with a witness**. Enter the witness's contact information and send the link. That's the whole setup — you don't need to export the file, email it, or find another way to get it to them.

Once they've reviewed it, revoke the link. There's no reason to leave it open after they no longer need it, and revoking takes one action from the same place you created it.

If there's anything about the recording you want your attorney or yourself to follow up on afterward — a detail the witness might raise, a date to double check — flag it while it's on your mind, so it doesn't get lost between now and the hearing.

## What Vero does for you

The link Vero creates opens only the record you chose. It does not give the witness access to your docket, your timeline, your other records, or anything else on the case — there is no broader account for them to log into.

Every link you create and every time you revoke one is recorded on the case itself, so there's always an accurate account of who saw what and when, without you having to keep track of it separately. If you're represented, your attorney sees this same record.
