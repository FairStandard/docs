A <span className="kw">witness</span> sometimes needs to see a specific record before they testify — a recording, a photo, a document — without getting access to the rest of your case. A witness link does exactly that and nothing more.

## Before you begin

Witness links depend on what your case has been set up with. If you don't see the option described below, your case has not been enabled for it yet.

1. Open the record you want to share.
2. Select **Share with a witness**.
3. Enter the witness's name and contact information.
4. Send the link.

The witness receives a link that opens only the record or records you selected — not your full case, not your docket, not anything else on the case.

## What happens next

The witness link stays active until you revoke it. You can revoke access at any time, for any reason, and the witness loses the ability to view the record immediately.

Every witness link you create or revoke is recorded on your case, so there is always a record of who saw what, and when.

If you're represented, your attorney sees the same list of witness links you do — the record of who has access is shared, not private to either of you.
