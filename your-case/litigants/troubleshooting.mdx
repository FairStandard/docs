Most problems on your case page come down to a case that hasn't been enabled for something yet, or a change that hasn't shown up on every screen at the same moment. The list below covers the ones people run into most.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A docket entry is missing or looks wrong">
    The docket mirrors the court's own register. If an event is missing, it usually means the court has not entered it yet rather than something Vero failed to pull in. If an entry looks wrong — a date, a result, a label — check it against the court's own record before assuming it's an error on your case, and flag it so it gets a second look.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My waiting-on list is empty">
    An empty waiting-on list means nothing is currently outstanding on your case. It refills as soon as something is owed to you or by you — it is not a sign the tracker has stopped working.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A flag I made isn't showing up for my attorney">
    Flags are shared the moment you submit them — there is no separate step to send a flag to your attorney. If your attorney says they don't see it, confirm you selected **Flag for review** and not a different action, and check that the flag is still open rather than already cleared.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't share evidence with a witness">
    Witness links depend on your case's setup. If **Share with a witness** doesn't appear on a record, your case has not been enabled for witness links yet — that's a fact about your case, not an error on your part.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My dashboard doesn't match what my attorney told me">
    Dashboard reflects the current state of the case, and both of you see the same one — there is no separate version for either of you. If something your attorney mentioned isn't showing yet, it usually hasn't been entered on the case record on their end. Check with them before assuming the dashboard is out of date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A court order I need isn't broken down into plain language yet">
    Order analysis works from orders that have already been added to your case. If a recent order isn't showing a plain-language breakdown yet, confirm it has been added to the case; a newly added order can take a short time to process.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck

If none of the above accounts for what you're seeing, contact [support@fairstandard.com](mailto:support@fairstandard.com) with your case name and a description of what you expected to see. Support can look at the case record directly rather than guessing from a description alone.
