Most problems on your case view come down to a case that hasn't been enabled for a capability yet, a case that belongs to a different organization than the one you're looking at, or a change that hasn't shown up on every screen at the same moment. The list below covers the ones people run into most.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A case I know exists isn't on my Cases list">
    Cases are scoped to the organization they were added under. If a case was added to a different firm you belong to, switch to that organization's context, or confirm you're actually a member of it — a case never appears under an organization it wasn't added to.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A docket entry is missing or looks wrong">
    The docket mirrors the court's own register. If an event is missing, it usually means the court hasn't entered it yet rather than something Vero failed to pull in. If an entry looks wrong — a date, a result, a label — check it against the court's own record before treating it as an error on the case.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My waiting-on queue looks incomplete">
    The queue rolls up every case you carry. If a case you expect to see is missing from it, confirm the case is still assigned to you and that its waiting-on tracker actually has something outstanding — an empty tracker on one case means nothing is currently owed there, not that the queue has stopped pulling it in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A client's flag isn't showing up on my flagged list">
    Flags are shared the moment a client submits them — there's no separate step that sends a flag to you. Confirm you're looking at the right case, and that the flag hasn't already been resolved and cleared by someone else on the matter.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Review tracking shows a document as unopened, but the client says they read it">
    Status updates when the client opens the item inside Vero. If they read a copy sent outside the platform, or opened it on a device where they weren't signed in, the status won't reflect that. Ask them to open it from their own account so the record is accurate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see order analysis or witness links on a case">
    Both are Advanced-tier capabilities enabled per case, not for your whole firm at once. If a button or page described elsewhere isn't on a specific case, that case hasn't been enabled for it — check the case's setup before assuming something is broken.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see workload and time estimation on my dashboard">
    This view is limited to owners and attorneys, and it's a Premium Professional capability. If your role or your firm's tier doesn't include it, the view won't appear, and that's a fact about your firm's setup rather than an error on your account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck

If none of the above accounts for what you're seeing, contact [support@fairstandard.com](mailto:support@fairstandard.com) with the case name, the organization it's under, and a description of what you expected to see. Support can look at the case record directly rather than guessing from a description alone.
