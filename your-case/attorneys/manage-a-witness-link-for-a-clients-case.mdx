A witness sometimes needs to see one record before they testify — a recording, a photo, a document — without getting access to the rest of the case. A witness link does exactly that and nothing more, and you can create or revoke one on a client's behalf.

## Before you begin

Witness links are an Advanced-tier capability enabled per case. If **Share with a witness** isn't on a record, the case hasn't been enabled for it yet.

1. Open the record on the client's case.
2. Select **Share with a witness**.
3. Enter the witness's name and contact information.
4. Send the link.

The witness receives a link that opens only the record or records you selected — not the case's docket, timeline, or anything else on the matter.

## What happens next

The link stays active until you revoke it. Revoke it the moment the witness no longer needs it — there's no reason to leave one open, and revoking takes one action from the same place you created it.

Every link you create or revoke is recorded on the case, so there's an accurate account of who saw what and when. Your client sees the same record of witness links you do.
