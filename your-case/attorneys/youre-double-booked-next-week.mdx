A hearing on one case and a deposition on another land on the same afternoon next week, and you didn't set either date yourself — the court set one, and a colleague scheduled the other.

## What to do

Open Dashboard. If workload and time estimation is enabled for your firm, the conflict shows up as a flagged item with a reason attached, not just a warning: "double-booked next week — the hearing overlaps the deposition," in the same style as every other ranking on the page. Open the docket on each case to confirm the exact times before you decide anything.

From there, either move the deposition, ask a colleague to cover the hearing, or ask the court for a continuance on the hearing if the deposition can't move. Whatever you decide, note it on the case so the record reflects what happened and why, not just the new date.

## What Vero does for you

Workload and time estimation looks across every case you carry, including matters at other firms you belong to, and surfaces a conflict before you'd otherwise notice it buried in two separate calendars. Rows drawn from more than one organization carry a Tag naming the firm, so a conflict between a case at one firm and a case at another is never presented as if both belonged to the same organization.

The dashboard doesn't just flag that two things overlap — it states which two, and why they conflict, the same way every other item on the page carries its reason rather than an unexplained score.
