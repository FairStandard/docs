Your case section is where you work every matter you carry, not one case at a time. Cases lists every matter you're on across every organization you belong to. Case Detail is the one page that holds everything about a single matter — the docket, the timeline, what's owed and what's outstanding, and what a client has flagged. Dashboard is the aggregated view that opens when you sign in, showing today's posture across your whole <span className="kw">caseload</span> in one place. This page explains what each screen shows and how they connect.

## How it works

A Case in Vero is one continuous record. Every court event, every record, and everything a client is waiting on attaches to the same Case, so the docket, the timeline, and the waiting-on tracker are three views of the same underlying facts rather than three lists you keep in sync by hand.

Capability access does not change by role. What changes is how the same information is organized: your view is dense and cross-case, built for someone who moves between matters all day, where a litigant's view of the identical Case is guided and case-centric. You and your client see the same underlying facts about a shared Case — neither of you has a simplified copy.

Each event, record, or order carries the court's own label and code where one exists, shown exactly as filed, next to a plain-language note on what it means for the matter.

## What you can do here

- **Track your caseload.** Caption, case number, court, parties, and phase for every matter you carry.
- **Investigate the full chronology.** The combined timeline merges court events and records into one sequence per case.
- **Work the docket.** Every filing, hearing, and service event, with results and notes, in the court's own words.
- **Run your work queue.** The waiting-on tracker rolls up what you owe and what you're owed across every case.
- **Track client review.** See whether a client has opened and gone through what you sent them.
- **Browse order analysis.** Parsed provisions from every order on a case, so you can catch a conflict between two orders or against a statute.
- **Work through flagged items.** A client's flag arrives with the record, docket entry, or finding it references already bundled.
- **Manage witness links.** Create and revoke a scoped link that lets a witness see one record without the rest of the case.
- **Check workload.** See your predicted workload across every firm you belong to, so double bookings surface early.
- **Check your dashboard.** One page rolls up today's posture across every matter you carry.

## Who sees what

Membership across organizations is many-to-many — you can practice at more than one firm at once, and what you see at one firm never leaks into another. Where a screen shows rows from more than one organization, such as your cross-firm dashboard or workload view, each row carries a Tag naming the firm it belongs to, so you always know which organization a case, a flag, or a deadline sits under before you act on it.

A client's flag is theirs to raise, not yours to dismiss unseen: it always arrives with its note and its bundled context intact. From there, whether an item needs your action, a staff member's follow-up, or nothing further is your call to make and record — the client's flag starts the review, it does not decide its outcome.

## Availability

Order analysis and witness links are Advanced-tier capabilities and are enabled per case, not for your whole firm at once — if a case hasn't been enabled, the corresponding page or button will not appear on it. Workload and time estimation is a Premium Professional capability, visible only to owners and attorneys, never to other staff roles. Each of these is a fact about a case's or a firm's setup, not a sign that something is broken.
