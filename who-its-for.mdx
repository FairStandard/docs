FairStandard is built for everyone involved in a legal case — not just attorneys. From the moment you create an account, your role shapes how the platform is presented and what guidance you receive, but it never restricts what you can do. Every user type has access to the same evidence engine, the same case workspace, the same Learning Center, and the same Marketplace. The difference is context and workflow, not capability.

<Note>
  Representation changes how FairStandard is presented — never what it can do. Every user has access to the same capabilities.
</Note>

## Self-Represented Litigants

If you're navigating a legal case without an attorney, FairStandard gives you the full toolkit that attorneys use — nothing locked out, nothing watered down. You can learn the process through the Learning Center, upload and organize all of your evidence, identify what strengthens your case, find patterns in communications and financial records, and prepare documents through to filing. The platform guides you through each step with plain-language explanations and jurisdiction-specific law assembled for your case type, so you're never left to figure out what comes next on your own.

## Represented Litigants

Working with an attorney doesn't mean stepping back from your own case. When you're represented, FairStandard gives you direct visibility into what's happening — your evidence, your timeline, your deadlines — and lets you do your own organizing, which means fewer billable hours spent on document review and intake. You can message your attorney directly within the platform, respond to requests, and track progress in real time. The platform keeps you informed and involved without duplicating or interfering with your attorney's work.

## Solo Attorneys & Small Firms

FairStandard gives solo practitioners and small firms the same leverage that large firms get from dedicated support staff and legal research teams. Assembled law and procedural guides for every jurisdiction and case type mean you spend less time on research and more time on strategy. Your clients join the platform alongside you, adopt it naturally because it's designed for them, and arrive at consultations better organized and more informed. The Marketplace surfaces your practice to litigants actively looking for representation in your area and case type.

## Legal Service Providers

Notaries, custody evaluators, mediators, forensic accountants, and other legal service professionals use FairStandard to make themselves findable and to run their engagements cleanly. Your Marketplace profile connects you with litigants and attorneys who need exactly what you offer. Within the platform, you get a focused slice of the workspace — the tools relevant to your consultation, without the full case management overhead. Scheduling, document handoff, and communication all happen inside the platform, tied to the case record where they belong.
