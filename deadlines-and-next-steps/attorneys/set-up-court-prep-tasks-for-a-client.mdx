A hearing goes more smoothly when a client has already done the small things it depends on — gathering a document, reading a specific lesson, reviewing a schedule. Court <span className="kw">prep</span> lets you build that list for a represented client rather than leaving them to guess what to do before the date arrives.

1. Open the case and find the upcoming hearing on its calendar.
2. On the hearing's detail view, open its prep task list.
3. Add a task and describe what it asks for — reviewing a document, gathering an exhibit, reading a specific lesson.
4. Save the task. It appears on the client's side of the same hearing, where they can open and complete it.
5. Repeat for each task the hearing needs. The hearing's detail view on your side shows how many are still open as the date approaches.

## Before you begin

This only applies to a represented client — someone with their own attorney of record on the case. A self-represented client's prep tasks tie to Learning Center lessons instead, and there's nothing for you to set on their hearing.

## What happens next

A task you set stays on the hearing's detail view after the client completes it, so you can confirm what was done before you walk into the hearing yourself. An incomplete task doesn't block anything else on the case — it stays visible as a plain record of what's still outstanding.
