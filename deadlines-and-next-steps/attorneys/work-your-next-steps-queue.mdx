Next Steps for you is not a view of any client's checklist — it's a distinct professional <span className="kw">queue</span> built from your own caseload: deadlines, filings, and client follow-ups you're responsible for, ranked by how soon each one matters.

1. From the sidebar, select **Next Steps**.
2. Items are ranked by urgency, not by case or date alone — a filing due in two days on one matter can outrank a hearing next week on another, and each item carries a line stating why.
3. Select any item to open the deadline, filing, or client follow-up behind it, on whichever case it belongs to.
4. Filter the queue to one case, one organization, or one kind of item if your caseload is large enough that the full list gets long.
5. Mark an item done as you complete it. Next Steps re-ranks itself as soon as the underlying deadline, filing, or follow-up changes — you don't have to refresh it by hand.

## Before you begin

Your queue only shows items from cases you're assigned to. If a case you expect to see isn't reflected here, confirm you've been added to it — an unassigned case never appears on your queue.

## What happens next

An item you complete stays visible on the case it came from, so there's a record of what was done and when. If a client is looking at the same underlying deadline from their side, their view stays separate — completing something on your queue doesn't check anything off theirs, and their activity doesn't check anything off yours.
