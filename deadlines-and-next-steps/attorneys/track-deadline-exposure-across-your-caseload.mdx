A caseload of any size has deadlines stacked on top of each other — a response due this week on one matter, a filing due next month on another. Vero tracks a Deadline's <span className="kw">urgency</span> the same way regardless of which case it's on, so exposure across your whole caseload reads as one ranked list rather than case by case.

1. From the sidebar, select **Calendar** or **Next Steps** — either shows deadline exposure, filtered differently.
2. On Calendar, every hearing, filing deadline, and response window across your caseload appears in date order, each tagged with the case and organization it belongs to.
3. Select any deadline to open its detail view. It shows the date it's due, a plain-language description, the rule or order behind it — quoted the way the court wrote it — and its current urgency status.
4. On Next Steps, the same deadlines appear ranked by urgency rather than by date: a response due in four days with nothing started outranks a hearing six weeks out that's already prepared for.
5. If you're an owner, deadline exposure also rolls up across the whole firm, not just your own assignments, so nothing slips through a gap between attorneys.

## Before you begin

You need to be added to a case before its deadlines appear in your exposure. A case with no events logged yet — no filings, orders, or hearings — starts with an empty deadline list, and that's expected for a new matter.

## What happens next

Once a deadline is visible, you can act on the case directly from its detail view, or find the same item ranked on your Next Steps queue with a reason attached. If the deadline's date changes because of a continuance or an amended order, it updates once, and everything tracking it — your queue, a reminder, a prep task tied to a hearing — moves with it.
