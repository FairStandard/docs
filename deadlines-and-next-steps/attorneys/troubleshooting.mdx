Most of what looks <span className="kw">stuck</span> here comes down to an event that hasn't been logged yet, or a case you haven't been added to. The list below covers what attorneys and firm staff run into most.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A case I'm on isn't showing its deadlines">
    Confirm you've been added to the case itself, not just to the firm. Deadline exposure only includes cases you're directly assigned to — being at the same firm as a case doesn't add it to your view on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A deadline I expect isn't on my calendar">
    A deadline only appears once the event that creates it — a filing, an order, a scheduled hearing — has been logged on the case. If you know a filing was made or an order was entered but don't see the deadline, check whether that event itself is on the docket yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My Next Steps queue doesn't match what I expect">
    The queue is ranked by urgency, not by date or by case, so an item you'd expect near the top can sit lower if it's already well underway. Open the item's detail view to confirm what's been completed toward it before assuming the ranking is wrong.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A court prep task I set for a client isn't showing on their side">
    Confirm the client is a represented party on the case, not self-represented — prep tasks you set only reach a represented client. A self-represented client's tasks tie to Learning Center lessons instead, and nothing you set will appear there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see firm-wide deadline exposure">
    Exposure aggregated across the whole firm is visible from an owner's view. If you're not seeing it, confirm your role on the organization — an attorney sees exposure across their own caseload, not the whole firm's, unless they're also an owner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A filing draft I shared isn't showing a client's review">
    Confirm the client opened the draft from their side of the case — a share doesn't force a review, it just makes the draft available. If they haven't opened it yet, the draft will show as shared but not yet reviewed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck

If none of the above accounts for what you're seeing, contact [support@fairstandard.com](mailto:support@fairstandard.com) with the case name and a description of what you expected. Support can look at the case record directly rather than guessing from a description alone.
