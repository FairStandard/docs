Discovery responses in Blake are due in 4 days — that's what a deadline this close looks like on your Next Steps queue, sitting ahead of almost everything else you're carrying this week. It <span className="kw">outranks</span> a hearing six weeks out on another matter, and the reason is stated right there rather than left for you to work out.

## What to do

Open the deadline from Next Steps and check its detail view — the plain-language description, the rule or order behind it, and how much of the response is already drafted. If nothing's started yet, that's exactly why it's ranked where it is; if a draft already exists, confirm it's ready to go out before the date arrives. Check whether the case has a filing workspace open for the response, or whether it needs to go out some other way.

## What Vero does for you

Next Steps re-ranks itself as the four days close in, and it re-ranks again the moment the response is filed or the date changes for any reason — a continuance, an amended order, a new filing on the same case. You don't have to keep checking the calendar to notice the window narrowing; the queue reflects it as soon as it happens. What Vero doesn't do is decide whether the response is adequate or draft the substance of it for you — that judgment stays with you.
