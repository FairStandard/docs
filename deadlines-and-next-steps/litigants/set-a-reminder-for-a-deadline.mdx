A deadline you have already seen once is easy to lose track of a month later. A <span className="kw">reminder</span> attaches to a specific deadline and reaches you again before it is due, on your terms rather than only when you happen to check your calendar.

1. Open Calendar or Next Steps and select the deadline you want a reminder for.
2. On the deadline's detail view, select **Set reminder**.
3. Choose how you want to hear about it — email, an in-app notification, or both.
4. Choose when. You can set more than one interval on the same deadline — for example, 14 days before and again 3 days before — so an early heads-up and a final nudge do not compete for one slot.
5. Save the reminder. It appears on the deadline's detail view from then on, and you can edit or remove it the same way.

## Before you begin

The deadline needs to already exist on your case before you can set a reminder for it — reminders attach to a deadline, they do not create one. If the deadline you are looking for is not on your calendar yet, see [Check your calendar for upcoming court dates](/deadlines-and-next-steps/litigants/check-your-calendar-for-upcoming-court-dates).

## What happens next

If the deadline's date changes — because of a continuance or an amended order — your reminder moves with it automatically, so you are not reminded about a date that no longer applies. If you set a reminder and later decide you do not need it, remove it from the same detail view; deleting a reminder does not touch the deadline itself.
