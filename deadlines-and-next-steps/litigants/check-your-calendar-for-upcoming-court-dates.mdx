Your case can have several dated obligations open at once — a hearing, a response window, a filing deadline — and they do not always sit next to each other on a single notice. Calendar puts every <span className="kw">hearing</span> and deadline on your case in one place, in date order, so you can see what is coming without checking several sources.

1. From Case Detail, select **Calendar**.
2. Scan the list in date order. Each row shows the date, a plain-language description of what is happening or due, and the court's own label for it where one exists.
3. Select any entry to open its detail view. This shows the full description, the rule or order that created it, and — for a hearing — the courtroom or location if one has been set.
4. If you only want to see one kind of entry, filter the list to hearings, filing deadlines, or response windows.
5. If a date looks wrong or missing, check whether the event it came from — a filing, an order, a continuance — has been logged on your case yet. A date that has not appeared usually means the triggering event has not been recorded, not that Vero skipped it.

## Before you begin

You need at least one event logged on your case — a filed motion, a scheduled hearing, or a court order — before anything appears on your calendar. If your case is brand new, this list starts empty and fills in as events are added.

## What happens next

Once you can see a date, you can act on it directly: set a reminder from its detail view, or find it again as an entry on your Next Steps list, where it is turned into a specific action rather than left as a date on its own. If the date is for a hearing and your case has court preparation turned on, the hearing's detail view also carries its own short list of prep tasks.
