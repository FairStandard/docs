A hearing is easier to walk into when you have already done the small things it depends on — gathering a document, reviewing a schedule, reading a lesson on what to expect. Court <span className="kw">prep</span> breaks that run-up into a short list of tasks tied to your hearing, rather than leaving you to remember everything on your own.

1. Open Calendar and select the upcoming hearing.
2. On the hearing's detail view, find its list of prep tasks.
3. Open a task to see what it asks for. If you are represented, some tasks may be set by your attorney; if you are self-represented, tasks are tied to relevant Learning Center lessons so you can read the background before doing the task.
4. Complete the task and mark it done. Vero explains legal process through these lessons — it does not tell you what to argue or how to answer a question in the hearing itself.
5. Repeat for each task on the list. The hearing's detail view shows how many are left as the date approaches.

## Before you begin

Court prep only appears on a hearing if your case has been set up with that capability. If a hearing on your calendar does not show a prep task list, that is a fact about your case's setup, not a mistake.

## What happens next

Completed tasks stay visible on the hearing's detail view after the hearing has passed, so you can see what was done to prepare for it later if you need to. An incomplete task does not block anything else on your case — it stays on the list as a plain reminder of what was not finished.

<Note>
  Vero explains legal process. It does not give legal advice.
</Note>
