Most of what looks <span className="kw">stuck</span> here comes down to an event that has not been logged yet, or a capability your case has not been set up with. The list below covers what people run into most.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A deadline I expect is not on my calendar">
    A deadline only appears once the event that creates it — a filing, an order, a scheduled hearing — has been logged on your case. If you know a filing was made or an order was entered but do not see the deadline yet, check whether that event itself is on your docket. If it is not there either, it has not been recorded on your case yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I set a reminder and did not get it">
    Confirm the reminder is still on the deadline's detail view — it should show the channel (email, in-app, or both) and the interval you chose. If the deadline's date changed after you set the reminder, the reminder moves with it, so check the current date rather than the one you remember setting it against.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I do not see court prep tasks on my hearing">
    Court preparation depends on what your case has been set up with. If a hearing's detail view has no prep task list, that is a fact about your case's setup, not a mistake — it does not affect anything else about the hearing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There is no filing workspace on Case Detail">
    Filing depends on what your case has been set up with, the same as court prep. If Case Detail does not show a filing workspace, your case has not been enabled for it yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Next Steps has not changed even though a deadline moved">
    Next Steps re-ranks itself when a deadline's date, or the events behind it, actually change on your case. If you expected a change and do not see one, check the deadline's detail view first to confirm the date itself moved — Next Steps reflects that date, it does not track anything separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A hearing shows the wrong location or time">
    Open the hearing and check the rule or order it is tied to — the detail view quotes the court's own wording for it. If the court itself changed the location or time and that has not reached your case yet, the change has not been logged as an event, and it will not update until it is.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck

If none of the above accounts for what you are seeing, contact [support@fairstandard.com](mailto:support@fairstandard.com) with your case name and a description of what you expected to see. Support can look at the case record directly rather than guessing from a description alone.
