A report pulls your records and findings together into one document, instead of leaving you to piece it together from separate screens.

## Before you begin

A report only includes what is already on your case, so add the records you want covered before you generate one.

1. Go to Reports.
2. Choose the kind of report you want — evidence over time, messages over time, or a timeline.
3. Select the date range you want the report to cover, or leave it open to include everything.
4. Select **Generate**.
5. Read the report on the page, or download it.

## What happens next

The report reflects your case as it stood at the moment you generated it. If you add a record afterward, generate a new report to include it — an old report does not update on its own, so the date it was generated is how you tell which version you are looking at.

Each report type pulls from a different slice of your case. An evidence-over-time report lays out what you added and when, so a gap in your uploads or a sudden burst of activity is visible at a glance. A messages-over-time report does the same for message exports and call recordings, which is useful when you want to show a pattern in communication rather than a single exchange. A timeline report combines your records with docket events into one chronology, similar to what you see on Case Detail, but formatted to read on its own without the rest of the page around it.

If you're represented, your attorney can generate the same reports from their side, working from the same records and findings you see. A report you generate does not change anything on the case itself — it is a read-only summary, so generating one, downloading it, or sharing it does not affect your records, your findings, or anyone else's access to them.
