You do not have to organize what you add. The Records Index <span className="kw">sorts</span> every record the moment it lands, and connects it to the rest of your case without you doing anything beyond adding it.

## What goes in the Records Index

Every record you add — a note, a photo, a voice memo, a message export, a call recording — sits alongside every document you store, such as court filings and financial records. Vero tags each one by type, by the date it happened or was added, and by the party it involves, so the same record can be found several different ways instead of only the one you happened to remember.

## Finding a record by event

Records connect to the docket event they belong with, when there is one. A recording from a custody exchange links to that exchange's date on your timeline; a document filed in response to a motion links to the motion itself. Browsing by event means you do not have to remember what a record was called or when you added it — you can start from the hearing or filing you already know about and find what belongs with it.

A record does not need an event to belong with in order to be useful. A note you wrote to yourself, a background bank statement, or a message export that does not tie to a specific hearing still sits in the Records Index, still gets tagged by type, date, and party, and can still be picked up by a finding. Event linking adds another way to find a record; it does not gate whether a record counts.

## What stays private and what does not

Everything you add to the Records Index is visible to everyone connected to the case in the usual way — if you're represented, that includes your attorney, the same as everything else on the case. A record does not have a separate, hidden state you can set on your own. If you need a witness to see one specific record without opening the rest of your case to them, that is a controlled link you create for that purpose, not a setting on the record itself; see [how your case page is organized](/your-case/litigants/how-your-case-page-is-organized) for how witness links work alongside the Records Index.
