Adding your first <span className="kw">record</span> and finding your way around the Records Index takes only a few minutes. This guide walks you through adding a record, browsing what is already there, and seeing what the analysis has found so far.

## What you'll learn

- How to add a record to your case
- Where to find your documents in the Records Index
- How to browse records by the event they relate to
- How to check your findings and generate a report

## Before you begin

You need a case set up in Vero before you can add anything to it. If you have not created your case yet, see [Get started with your case](/your-case/litigants/get-started) first.

### Step 1: Add a record

From Case Detail, select **Add a record**. Choose what you are adding — a note, a photo, a voice memo, a message export, or a call recording — and upload it. There is no need to explain why it matters before you add it; a short note is optional.

### Step 2: Store a document

Court filings, financial records, and anything else you already have as a file go through the same **Add a record** button. Once it is added, it appears in the Records Index, open for you to read whenever you need it.

### Step 3: Browse the Records Index

Open the Records Index. Records appear in the order you added them, and each one can be filtered by the hearing or filing it relates to, so you can find what you need without scrolling through everything you have added.

### Step 4: Check your findings

Go to Case Detail and open findings. Each one is a plain sentence describing what Vero noticed, with the records it drew from listed next to it, so you can check the source before deciding what to do about it.

### Step 5: Generate a report

Go to Reports and choose what you want to see — evidence over time, messages over time, or a timeline. Vero builds the report from your records and findings and shows it on the page, ready to read straight through.

## Tips

- Add records as you get them rather than saving them up. A record added the day you receive it is easier to place on the timeline than one added months later from memory.
- If you are not sure whether something is relevant, add it anyway. Vero decides what it connects to; you do not need to make that call yourself.
