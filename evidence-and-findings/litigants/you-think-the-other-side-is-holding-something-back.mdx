You asked for something in discovery — the formal process where each side must produce specific documents and records to the other — and it still has not shown up. You are not sure whether it was missed, refused, or simply has not been sent yet.

## What to do

Open the discovery checklist on Case Detail. It lists each item requested — often through a formal Request for Production, or "RFP" — and shows its status: produced, outstanding, or objected. Find the item you are missing and check its status before assuming nothing has happened; an item can be marked as objected rather than simply missing, and that is a different situation with a different next step. If the item is still outstanding past the date it was due, flag it so it gets a closer look, whether that look is yours or your attorney's.

## What Vero does for you

Vero tracks what has been requested and what has come in for each request set, so the checklist reflects the actual state of discovery rather than your memory of it. It does not tell you whether an objection is valid or what to file if an item is genuinely missing — those are legal judgment calls, and they depend on facts the checklist alone cannot capture, like whether the deadline was extended or the request was too broad to begin with. If you are self-represented, deciding what to do about a missing item is yours to make; if you are represented, your attorney sees the same checklist and the same status you do.

</Note>
