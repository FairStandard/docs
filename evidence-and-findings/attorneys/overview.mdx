Evidence and findings holds every document, record, and finding attached to a case, plus the reports built from them. Documents is the firm's file store for a case, with visibility controls attached to each item. Case Detail is where the underlying <span className="kw">corpus</span> gets read: findings, patterns, and discovery completeness sit here next to the docket and the timeline. Reports turns any of it into a document you can generate on demand, for one case or across your caseload. This page explains what each screen shows and how they connect.

## How it works

A record is anything captured about a case — a note, a photo, a voice memo, a message export, a call recording, or a filed document. Once a record is added, analysis reads it, tags it, and connects it to the rest of the case: the docket event it relates to, the party it involves, the other records it matches or contradicts. A finding states what a group of records shows, and carries its attribution, the defenses raised against it, a confidence score, and its place in the review queue, so nothing reaches the queue as an unexplained conclusion.

Related findings cluster into patterns — allegations, denials, admissions, and non-responses grouped as repeated conduct rather than a single incident. Discovery completeness tracks the same records against what each request set actually required, so a request shows as produced, outstanding, or objected rather than simply present or missing.

Capability access does not change by case relationship. A client sees the same records and the same findings you do — there is no reduced copy of the corpus kept for staff. Where a case is self-represented, the client decides which findings need a closer, human look; where a case is represented, that call is yours, and the review queue is where you make it.

## What you can do here

- **Manage case documents.** Set a document's visibility — standard, hidden, or sealed — and keep it categorized so access stays controlled.
- **Find documents by court event.** Browse by the hearing or filing a document belongs to instead of scrolling a flat list.
- **Browse the case's evidence corpus.** Everything a client has captured, verified, and organized in one place.
- **Work the findings matrix.** Attribution, defenses, confidence, and a review queue for every finding on a case.
- **Read clustered patterns.** Claims grouped as allegations, denials, admissions, or non-responses.
- **Track discovery completeness.** See exactly which items are produced, outstanding, or objected, per request set.
- **Use measured communication signals.** Emotion scores, baselines, and change points, treated as evidence-grade measurements rather than impressions.
- **Check speaker identification.** Confirm how each voice in a recording was matched to a person before you rely on it.
- **Generate reports.** Build the report catalog for a case or across your practice — evidence over time, messages over time, or a timeline.

## Who sees what

Membership across organizations is many-to-many: you can practice at more than one firm, and where a screen shows records or findings from more than one organization, each row carries a Tag naming the firm it belongs to, so you always know whose corpus you're looking at before you rely on it.

A client sees the same records, findings matrix, and discovery checklist you do — there is no simplified copy held back from them. A flag arrives with its note and context already bundled, but it only opens the review. On a self-represented case, the client decides which flagged items need a closer look. On a represented case, that decision is yours to make.

## Availability

Documents, the evidence corpus, and Reports are Basic tier, available on every case. Findings and pattern analysis are Premium Professional, enabled per case. Discovery completeness is Advanced tier, also enabled per case. Communication measurements and speaker identification are Premium Professional, priced by usage or a flat bundle, and enabled per case rather than firm-wide. A capability missing from a case is a fact about that case's setup, not a sign of a problem.
