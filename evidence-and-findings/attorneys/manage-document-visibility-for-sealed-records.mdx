Not every document that belongs on a case should be visible to everyone on it. Setting a <span className="kw">sealed</span> or hidden state keeps a document on the case record without putting it in front of a client who shouldn't see it yet.

1. **Open Documents for the case.** Every uploaded or received item appears here, with its current visibility state shown.
2. **Open a document and select its visibility.** Choose standard, hidden, or sealed depending on who should be able to open it.
3. **Set a category.** Categorizing a document — financial, correspondence, filing, and so on — keeps the corpus organized as it grows.
4. **Confirm who can see it.** Check the document's visibility state before you rely on it staying restricted; a document set to standard is visible to everyone with access to the case, including a represented client.

## What happens next

A document's visibility state applies immediately and stays in place until it's changed again. A client never sees a hidden or sealed document, and a hidden or sealed state doesn't remove the document from the corpus — it stays attached to the case and can still be included in a finding or a report, subject to the same visibility rule.
