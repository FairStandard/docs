A busy case and a quiet one don't need the same alerts, so notification preferences are set per case rather than once for your whole account — which means a message on a matter with a hearing next week can reach you by email while a slower matter stays to an in-app notice. This walks you through setting them so you're never <span className="kw">alerted</span> more, or less, than a case warrants.

## Before you begin

You need at least one case assigned to you before there's anything to set preferences on.

1. Open your account settings and select **Notifications**.
2. You'll see every case you're on, listed separately rather than as one account-wide toggle.
3. For each case, choose email, in-app, or both for new messages.
4. Repeat for any other case where you want a different combination.
5. Save your changes.

## What happens next

Your new preferences apply to messages sent after you save them; they don't change how anything already sent was delivered. If you turn off email for a case, you'll still see new messages the next time you open Vero — turning off a channel changes how you're told about a message, not whether it exists. A client's preferences on the same case are set independently of yours; turning off email for yourself doesn't change what they receive, and their setting doesn't change yours.
