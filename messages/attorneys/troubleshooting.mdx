Most problems here come down to a case you haven't been added to, a preference set on the wrong case, or a <span className="kw">missing</span> feature you haven't turned on rather than a message that failed to send. The list below covers what attorneys and firm staff run into most.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A case's threads aren't showing in my inbox">
    Confirm you've been added to the case itself, not just to the firm. Your inbox only includes cases you're directly assigned to — belonging to the same firm as a case doesn't add its threads to your view on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a reply I was told was sent">
    Check the thread directly rather than relying on a notification — a reply can be sitting in Vero even if an alert about it didn't reach you. If email is off for that case, that's the most common reason a reply feels missing when it isn't.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm not getting notified about a specific case">
    Notification preferences are set per case, not once for your whole account. Check the preference for that case specifically; a channel you turned on for one matter doesn't carry over to another.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A firm-internal note is showing up for the client">
    Confirm you used the internal note option and not the message composer — the two live on the same thread, but only the note is kept from the client. If a note appears on the client's side, it was sent as a message, not left as a note.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't attach a record to a message">
    You can only attach a record that's already on the case. If the document isn't showing up in the attachment picker, it hasn't been added to the case yet — add it there first, then attach it to the message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a cooperation picture on a case">
    Cooperation tracking is an add-on, not something every case has. If it isn't showing on a case's Messages screen, that case hasn't been enabled for it yet — that's a fact about the case's setup, not an error.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see firm-wide cooperation trends">
    A firm-wide roll-up is visible from an owner's view once at least one case in the firm has tracking turned on. If you're not seeing it, confirm your role on the organization — an attorney sees cooperation signals for their own caseload, not the whole firm's, unless they're also an owner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck

If none of the above accounts for what you're seeing, contact [support@fairstandard.com](mailto:support@fairstandard.com) with the case name and a description of what you expected to see. Support can look at the case record directly rather than guessing from a description alone.
