Messages works the same way it does on a single case, but scoped to everything you carry — one <span className="kw">inbox</span> for every client, across every organization you belong to, instead of a separate mailbox per matter. This page explains what each part shows and how a thread stays tied to the case and firm it belongs to.

## How it works

Every conversation still lives inside a thread tied to one case, organized by topic rather than dumped into a flat feed. What changes for you is the view above the threads: your inbox rolls up every case you're assigned to, so a message from a client at one firm and a message from a client at another sit in the same list, each carrying a Tag naming the organization it belongs to. You never have to guess which matter a thread is attached to before you act on it.

You can attach a record already on a case directly to a message, from wherever it lives on that case. The attachment points to the existing record instead of creating a copy, so the client reading the message sees it in the same context you do.

If a case has cooperation tracking turned on, the same messages and deadlines feed a picture of how communication is going on that case — response times, and whether anything a court order required was met. That picture is built per case; there's no single number that rolls up across your whole caseload the way your inbox does, though a firm owner can see the trend across every case the firm carries.

Vero tells you when something needs attention instead of leaving you to check back on your own. A new message, a reply, or a case update can trigger an alert by email, an in-app notice, or both, set per case rather than once for your whole account.

## What you can do here

- **Message a client across your caseload.** One inbox for every case you're on, instead of switching between them to find a thread.
- **Keep conversations organized by case and topic.** A thread stays filed under the case and subject it started with.
- **Attach a record.** Point to a document already on a case instead of re-uploading it into a message.
- **Leave a firm-internal note on a thread.** A note visible to colleagues at your firm, kept separate from what the client on the thread sees.
- **Get alerted across your caseload.** Choose email, an in-app notice, or both, set per case.
- **See how communication is going on a case.** If cooperation tracking is turned on, response times and requirement compliance sit alongside the thread.

## Who sees what

A client sees the same thread you do — messaging follows the case relationship, so there's no separate channel that leaves either of you out of a conversation you're both party to. A firm-internal note attached to that thread is the exception: your client never sees it, because it's a coordination note for colleagues at your firm, not part of the conversation with the client.

Cross-organization boundaries hold everywhere else. A thread on a case at one firm never appears in your inbox for another firm you belong to, even though both sit in the same list — each carries a Tag naming its organization, and that Tag is how you tell them apart rather than a guess based on the client's name. A client's own inbox never shows threads from a case they aren't on, and it never shows a firm-internal note at all.

## Availability

Cooperation tracking is an add-on, turned on per case rather than by default across a firm. If it isn't showing on a case's Messages screen, that case hasn't been enabled for it yet — that's a fact about the case's setup, not a mistake, and it doesn't affect sending or receiving messages on it. A firm-wide roll-up of cooperation trends is visible from an owner's view once at least one case in the firm has tracking turned on.
