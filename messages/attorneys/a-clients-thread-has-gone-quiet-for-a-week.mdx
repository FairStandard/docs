Aug 5, 2026 · 7 days quiet — that's what a thread with no reply looks like when you check it between two other matters. A <span className="kw">quiet</span> thread on one case isn't unusual by itself, but with a caseload of threads to watch, it's worth a quick check before you decide whether it's ordinary or worth raising.

## What to do

Open the thread and check when your message actually sent — a message drafted last week but only sent yesterday hasn't had time for a reply. Check the client's notification preferences for the case too; if email is off, a reply might already be sitting in Vero without an alert reaching either of you. If the case has cooperation tracking turned on, check the response-time trend before you follow up — a single quiet week can be ordinary for a client who is otherwise prompt, or it can be the second one in a row.

## What Vero does for you

Vero doesn't guess at why a reply hasn't come and doesn't send a follow-up on your behalf. What it does track, on a case with cooperation tracking turned on, is how response times compare over time, so a slow week shows up as part of a trend instead of disappearing once a reply finally arrives — and across your caseload, that trend surfaces alongside every other case you're watching rather than requiring you to check each one in turn.
