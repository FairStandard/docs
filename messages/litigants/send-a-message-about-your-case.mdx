A message about your case starts as a thread with a subject, not a blank line in a shared inbox, so the person you're messaging knows what it's about before they even open it. This walks you through sending one and <span className="kw">attaching</span> a record if you need to.

## Before you begin

You need at least one attorney or legal provider connected to your case. If no one is connected yet, there's no one on the other end of a thread you start.

1. From your case, select **Messages**.
2. Select **New thread**.
3. Give the thread a short subject — the document, deadline, or question it's about.
4. Write your message in the composer.
5. If your message is about a document already on your case, select the attachment option and choose the record from your case instead of uploading it again.
6. Select **Send**.

## What happens next

The person you messaged is notified according to their own preferences — by email, an in-app notice, or both. Your message stays in the thread you started it in, so if you or they reply later, the reply lands in the same place instead of starting a new, disconnected conversation.

If you attached a record, the recipient opens the same file you have on your case. There's no separate copy for either of you to keep track of, and any note you added to the record on your case still sits with the original, not just with the copy you sent.
