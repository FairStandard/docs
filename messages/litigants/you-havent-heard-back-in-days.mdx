Aug 5, 2026 · 4 days quiet — that is what a thread with no reply looks like when you check it. A <span className="kw">quiet</span> thread is not the same as a dropped one, but it's worth a quick check before you assume either way.

## What to do

Open the thread and check when your message actually sent, not just when you wrote it — a message you started drafting a week ago but only sent yesterday hasn't had time for a reply yet. Check your notification preferences too; if email is off for this case, a reply might already be sitting in Vero without an alert reaching you. If the thread is genuinely quiet and the person you're waiting on is your attorney, a short follow-up message in the same thread is enough — you don't need to start a new one.

## What Vero does for you

Vero doesn't guess at why a reply hasn't come and doesn't chase anyone on your behalf. What it does track, if your case has cooperation tracking turned on, is how response times on your case compare over time, so a slow week shows up as part of a pattern instead of disappearing once the reply finally arrives. That picture can tell you whether four quiet days is ordinary for this case or a change worth mentioning.
