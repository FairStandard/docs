New messages and updates on your case can reach you by email, an in-app notice, or both, whichever combination means you actually see them, rather than <span className="kw">missing</span> a reply because you weren't checking one channel.

## Before you begin

Notification preferences are set per case, not once for your whole account, so if you have more than one active case, plan to repeat this for each one.

1. Open your account settings and select **Notifications**.
2. Find the messages category for the case you want to change.
3. Choose email, in-app, or both.
4. Repeat for any other active case, since preferences don't carry over between cases.
5. Save your changes.

## What happens next

Your new preferences apply to messages sent after you save them; they don't change how anything already sent was delivered. If you turn off email for a case, you'll still see new messages the next time you open Vero — turning off a channel changes how you're told about a message, not whether it exists.

If you're represented and your attorney sends something urgent, they can't force a notification through a channel you've turned off. Checking Messages directly is the reliable way to catch something on a case where you've narrowed your alerts down to one channel.
