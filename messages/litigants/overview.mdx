Messages holds every conversation about your case in one place — <span className="kw">threads</span> with your attorney or a legal provider, alerts about what changed, and, once your case has it turned on, a simple picture of how communication between everyone on the case is going. This page explains what each part shows and how they connect.

## How it works

Every conversation on your case lives inside a thread, not a flat inbox you have to scroll through. You start a thread about a specific document, a deadline, or a question, and it stays organized under that topic instead of mixing with everything else you've discussed. A thread you started about a hearing next month is easy to find weeks later, because it's filed under that hearing rather than buried between unrelated messages.

You can attach a record already on your case directly to a message, from wherever it lives on your case. The attachment links to the existing record instead of creating a copy, so the person reading the message sees it in the same context you do, with nothing to re-upload.

Vero tells you when something needs your attention instead of leaving you to check back on your own. A new message, a reply, or a change on your case can trigger an alert by email, an in-app notice, or both, depending on what you've chosen for that case.

If your case has cooperation tracking turned on, the same messages and deadlines feed a simple picture of how communication is going — how quickly things get answered, and whether anything a court order required was missed.

## What you can do here

- **Send a message.** Reach your attorney or a legal provider connected to your case directly, without leaving Vero for email or text.
- **Keep conversations organized by topic.** Start a thread for a specific document, deadline, or question, and find it again by what it's about.
- **Attach a record.** Point to a document already on your case instead of re-uploading it into a message.
- **Get alerted when something changes.** Choose email, an in-app notice, or both for new messages and other updates.
- **See how communication is going.** If your case has it turned on, a plain picture of response times and any missed requirements sits alongside your threads.

## Who sees what

Messaging follows your case relationship, not your case type. If you're represented, your attorney sees the same threads you do — there's no separate channel that leaves either of you out. If you're self-represented, your threads are with whichever legal provider or support contact you've connected to your case, and they work the same way.

## Availability

The cooperation picture depends on what your case has been set up with. If it isn't showing on your Messages screen, your case hasn't been enabled for it yet — that's a fact about your case's setup, not a mistake, and it doesn't affect your ability to send or receive messages.
