Most problems with Messages come down to a case that hasn't been enabled for something yet, or a <span className="kw">delayed</span> notification rather than a missing message. The list below covers what people run into most.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a reply I was told was sent">
    Check the thread directly rather than relying on a notification — a reply can be sitting in Vero even if an alert about it didn't reach you. If email is turned off for this case, that's the most common reason a reply feels missing when it isn't.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm not getting notified about new messages">
    Confirm your notification preferences for this case; they're set per case, not once for your whole account. If they're already on and you still aren't seeing alerts, check the email address on file for typos.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't attach a record to a message">
    You can only attach a record that's already on your case. If the document you want isn't showing up in the attachment picker, it hasn't been added to your case yet — add it there first, then attach it to the message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My message went to the wrong thread">
    A message always sends into the thread you had open when you sent it. If it landed somewhere unexpected, check which thread was open before you started typing; starting a new thread with a clear subject avoids this going forward.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a cooperation picture on my case">
    Cooperation tracking is an add-on, not something every case has. If it isn't showing on your Messages screen, your case hasn't been enabled for it yet — that's a fact about your case's setup, not an error.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck

If none of the above accounts for what you're seeing, contact [support@fairstandard.com](mailto:support@fairstandard.com) with your case name and a description of what you expected to see. Support can look at the case record directly rather than guessing from a description alone.
