Getting comfortable with Messages takes a few minutes. This guide walks you through sending your first message, starting a thread, attaching a record, and choosing how you want to be <span className="kw">notified</span> when something changes.

## What you'll learn

- How to send your first message
- How threads keep conversations organized by topic
- How to attach a record from your case to a message
- How to set your notification preferences
- Where your cooperation picture shows up, if your case has it turned on

## Before you begin

You need a case set up in Vero, and someone connected to it — an attorney or a legal provider — before there's anyone to message. If you haven't created your case yet, see [Get started with your case](/your-case/litigants/get-started) first.

### Step 1: Open Messages

From your case, select **Messages**. If this is your first message, you'll see an empty thread list rather than an inbox full of unrelated conversations — that's expected, not a problem.

### Step 2: Start a thread

Select **New thread** and give it a short subject, such as the document, deadline, or question it's about. Everything you send in that thread stays grouped under the subject you gave it.

### Step 3: Write and send your message

Type your message and select **Send**. Something like `"Can we go over the exhibit list before Thursday?"` sends the same way any message does — the person on the other end is notified according to their own preferences.

### Step 4: Attach a record

If your message is about a document already on your case, select the attachment option in the message composer and choose the record from your case rather than uploading it again. The recipient opens the same record you have, in the same context.

### Step 5: Set your notification preferences

Go to your notification settings and choose email, an in-app notice, or both for new messages. You can set different preferences for each case if you have more than one.

### Step 6: Check your cooperation picture

If your case has cooperation tracking turned on, open the picture from Messages to see how quickly things are getting answered on your case. If you don't see it, your case hasn't been enabled for it yet.

## Tips

- Give each thread a subject specific enough to find later — "exhibit list" is easier to locate in three months than "question."
- Set your notification preferences before you need them. Waiting until you've missed a reply is the wrong time to discover email notifications were off.
