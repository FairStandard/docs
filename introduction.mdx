Upload your documents, recordings, and correspondence, and FairStandard organizes everything automatically, surfaces what matters, and guides you from the first intake step all the way through filing. No matter where you are in the process, every tool you need is in one place.

## What FairStandard Does

At the core of FairStandard is a document and evidence engine built around a single principle: give us everything, and we'll find what wins. Upload recordings, text message exports, court orders, financial documents, and opposing filings — the engine ingests them all, organizes them automatically, and identifies what matters. That means behavioral patterns across communications, hidden or misrepresented assets in financial records, violations of existing court orders, and gaps in opposing disclosures — all surfaced, cited, and ready to use.

That engine powers five interconnected surfaces. The **case workspace** is your command center: assembled law for your jurisdiction, a full evidence timeline, upcoming deadlines, and recommended next steps. **Messaging** keeps communication with your attorney or team inside the platform and tied to your case record. The **deadline and next-steps tracker** tells you what needs to happen and when, so nothing falls through the cracks. The **Learning Center** explains the legal process in plain language, organized by jurisdiction and case type, so you always know what you're walking into. And the **Marketplace** connects you with attorneys, notaries, evaluators, and mediators — vetted and available when you need them.
