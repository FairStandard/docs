Date: 2026-08-30

## Status

Accepted

## Context

FairStandard serves the whole legal industry, family law first, across
states (canon: capability parity, four-axis audiences). Every state has
its own family code, its own court rules, its own pro bono thresholds.
Building law-holding logic per state, or per practice area, means a new
service every time a state or a rule family is added — a scaling
approach that fails the moment the second state onboards.

## Decision

Jurisdiction law is one content service, not one service per state.
Everything a state contributes (statutes now; court rules, process
definitions, and pro bono eligibility later — named as owned-here in
CONTEXT.md, not built yet) is content this service holds, partitioned by
jurisdiction. Onboarding a new state is a data upload against this
service's bulk-load door, never a new deployment, never new code.

## Consequences

Adding Wave 2's court rules and process definitions is an additive slice
inside this service rather than a new service. The service's own scaling
axis is jurisdiction count and content volume per jurisdiction, not
number of states deployed.
