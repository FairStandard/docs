---
title: "2. Event-fed only; this service never calls out"
description: "Date: 2026-08-30"
---

Date: 2026-08-30

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Search must not reach into other services' private databases (the
platform's own-Postgres-each ruling), and coupling it to their APIs
would make it a hub that changes whenever they do.

## Decision

Every document search-api knows about arrives as an `IndexFact` on this
service's own stream (a servicekit `StreamQueue`): producers publish
what they want searchable, and the indexer consumes and upserts. This
service exposes exactly two doors — search and delete-by-entity — and
initiates no calls to any other service. Evidence stays a leaf: search
only ever touches its own event stream and its own index.

## Consequences

Producers stay decoupled (publish and move on); search-api stays a leaf
on the call graph; replaying the fact stream can rebuild the index from
scratch if it's ever lost.
