Date: 2026-08-30

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The ported format parsers cover exactly the exports Christopher happened
to have. Real intake is wider: a screenshot of iPhone call history IS a
call record; a screenshot of a message thread IS a message log; a screen
recording scrolled through messages IS a message log; a video of a paper
document being flipped through IS a record. The container (PDF, image,
video, CSV) says how to read the file; the meaning (call record, message
log, court document) says what must come out of it. Christopher's ruling
2026-08-30: "REGARDLESS OF HOW THE PDF IS LAID OUT, we have to get the
right data off the page," extended to every container.

## Decision

The ingest engine is the one front door, and its pipeline is
modality-agnostic:

1. Fingerprint, classify the container, and register. Classification's
   container verdict doubles as the registration's media type, so the
   classify call runs first; an already-delivered duplicate stops here,
   while a re-upload whose first attempt failed or was flagged is
   retried — "duplicate" is only honest about files that actually made
   it in.
2. Classify the MEANING: deterministic rules first (bytes and names —
   the existing classifier); an AI meaning tier may decide only when the
   rules cannot. This softens the earlier "never by AI" stance to
   deterministic-first, the same cost law as extraction.
3. Extract: the deterministic processor when one fits the exact format;
   the AI extraction tier otherwise (document reader today; image and
   frame-by-frame video readers are additive tiers behind the same
   Extractor seam).
4. Validate per ADR 0004; failures escalate; still-invalid imports are
   flagged for review. Nothing invalid is delivered silently.
5. Resolve parties: phone numbers and handles in extracted records are
   cross-referenced against the case's known parties and rewritten to
   canonical names; the engine injects case and workspace identity.
6. Deliver to the evidence store and append to the audit chain.

Format parsers are only the cheap fast lane. Adding a reader for a new
container or format is additive — a new processor registration or a new
extractor tier — and never moves the engine's seams.

Scope note: this slice ships the engine with the deterministic meaning
tier and the document-reading AI extraction tier. The AI meaning tier and
the image and frame-by-frame video extraction tiers are additive
follow-ups behind the same seams — no delivery path may be built that
bypasses the engine while waiting for them.

## Consequences

Every delivery path goes through one engine, so ADR 0004's
validate-escalate-flag law is enforced mechanically rather than by
convention. The AI meaning tier and the image/video extraction tiers can
land later without touching callers. Party attribution stops depending on
what a specific export happens to print.
