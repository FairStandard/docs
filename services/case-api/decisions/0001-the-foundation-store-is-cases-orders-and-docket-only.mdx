Date: 2026-08-30

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Wave 1 (FS-857) carves case-api out of the platform as its own service with
its own Postgres. The map ruled six services, each owning one slice.
Adjacent slices are explicitly out of scope for this land: case-team
records belong to IDENTITY (ruled 2026-08-30 — case-api must never create
case-team tables), deadlines get their own service later, and
cooperation/violations plus discovery tracking are later slices on this
same repo.

## Decision

This slice ships four tables and nothing else:

- `Case` — caption, case number, court, phase.
- `CaseParty` — the caption parties (petitioner/respondent and similar),
  scoped to a case. This is deliberately not the same thing as a
  case-team record (who is working the case, which org, which role) —
  those stay in IDENTITY. A caption party is a fact about the matter
  itself, not about who represents anyone.
- `CourtOrder` — one order, its status, and free-text notes.
- `DocketEntry` — the event register: filings, hearings, service, with a
  result and notes.

Every table carries `workspace_id`, and every store method takes it as the
first argument and filters on it — a row from another workspace does not
exist as far as the query is concerned, not just as far as the response
is concerned.

## Consequences

Building deadlines, cooperation, or discovery tracking later means adding
new tables and routes to this same service, not touching these four. If
IDENTITY's case-team model changes shape, case-api's tables are
unaffected — they hold no team data to begin with.
