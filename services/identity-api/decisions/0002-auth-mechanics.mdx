Ruled 2026-08-30. This service ports the SHAPE of the monolith's auth
mechanics (`platform/apps/api/app/services/auth.py`,
`platform/apps/api/app/models/pg/session.py`) onto its own Postgres, with
no Mongo, no legacy dual-token support, and no RLS.

- **Passwords** are hashed with bcrypt. Plaintext never touches storage or
  logs.
- **Access tokens** are JWTs signed with HS256, carrying `sub` (the
  account id), `iat`, and `exp`. The secret and expiry live in
  `Settings` under the `IDENTITY_` prefix.
- **Sessions are the revocation mechanism.** A JWT being unexpired is not
  enough to prove an account is still logged in — a matching `Session`
  row (sha256 of the token, an expiry) must also exist. `/internal/verify`
  checks both. Logging out deletes the session row, which revokes the
  token immediately even though the JWT itself would still decode as
  valid until its own `exp`.
- **No SSE-purpose tokens in this service.** The monolith's short-lived
  `purpose: sse` token exists because its API streams over SSE directly;
  that concern belongs to whichever downstream service serves a stream,
  not to identity. If a future caller needs one, it is a small addition
  to `auth.py`'s pure functions, not a redesign.

`auth.py` stays pure functions (hash/verify password, create/decode JWT,
hash a token for storage) with no database access, so it is trivial to
unit test without Postgres. `store.py` is the only place that touches
`Session` rows.
