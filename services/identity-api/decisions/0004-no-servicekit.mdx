Ruled 2026-08-31 (recorded during the estate audit remediation; the skip
itself dates from the scaffold and was never written down — that was the
gap, not the decision).

The estate standard is: pin servicekit at an exact rev where adopted, or
document the skip. identity-api skips it, on three grounds:

- **No queue.** This service consumes no stream and publishes none.
  `StreamQueue` and its codecs have nothing to do here.
- **No outbound calls.** identity-api calls no other service — it is the
  one every other service calls (`/internal/verify`). The doorway
  contract (`call_with_retries`, `Rejected`/`Unavailable`) exists for
  callers; this service has no caller side.
- **One header does not justify a dependency.** The single
  `X-Workspace-Id` read on the case-team door is one `Header(alias=...)`
  annotation. Pulling in `servicekit.fastapi` for that would add a
  pinned git dependency to save one line.

Revisit when any of the three change — the moment identity needs a
stream, an outbound doorway, or grows a second workspace-scoped door,
adoption stops being over-engineering and the pin rule applies as
written.

The CI workflow originally carried a `SERVICEKIT_TOKEN` git-access step
copied from the estate scaffold; nothing in this repo's dependency graph
needs it, and it was removed the day this ADR was written.
