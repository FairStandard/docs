---
title: "3. Facts flow in; this service never calls out"
description: "Date: 2026-08-30"
---

Date: 2026-08-30

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The relations service must not reach into other services' private
databases (the platform's own-Postgres-each ruling), and coupling it to
their APIs would make it a hub that changes when they do.

## Decision

Steady-state edges arrive as Facts on THIS service's own fact stream:
producers write assertions into it through the shared vocabulary
package's publisher (the one write door they get — they never see the
consumer side), and this service's projector consumes and writes Edges.
The stream is private in the sense that matters: only this service reads
it, and nothing else ever queries the edges except through the read
doors. This service exposes reads and the
bulk-load door; it initiates no calls to any other service.

## Consequences

Producers stay decoupled (they publish and move on), the relations
service stays a leaf on the call graph, and replaying facts can rebuild
the graph.
