Date: 2026-08-30

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Christopher's correction on the messaging carve: there must be a clear
seam between messaging/communication and the knowledge of which files
relate to which. Per-service relationship tables fail once a tie spans
services (the same person across records and findings has no natural
owner). Research (scratch_pads/relations-research/findings.md) ruled out
the embedded-engine route (its one credible engine was archived by its
maintainers) and the dedicated graph databases (licensing).

## Decision

Relationship knowledge is its own small service. It stores typed Edges
between References — (type, id) pairs — and never learns any other
service's schema. The relation vocabulary lives in a shared package all
services import, so the kinds are centrally defined even though only
this service stores the data. Messaging, and everyone else, composes
this service's answers and owns no relationship logic.

## Consequences

New relation kinds and new consumers are additive. The messaging seam
Christopher required exists structurally: conversations live there,
connections live here.
