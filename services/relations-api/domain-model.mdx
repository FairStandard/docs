**Edge**:
One typed relationship fact: a subject (type, id), an object (type, id),
and a relation kind — "this record is an attachment of that message."
Edges hold identifiers only, never another service's data.
_Avoid_: link (messaging-api owns that word for its bundles), relationship (bare)

**Relation kind**:
The vocabulary of ways two things can relate (attachment_of, same_thread,
annotated_by, flagged_by_finding, mentions_same_party...). Defined once in
the shared vocabulary package every service imports — never invented ad
hoc at a call site.
_Avoid_: edge type (kind is the field name), label

**Reference**:
A (type, id) pair naming a thing another service owns — a record, a file,
a finding, a note, a party. The relations service knows nothing else
about it.
_Avoid_: pointer, foreign key

**Fact**:
A relationship assertion published by the service that discovered it,
consumed by this service's projector and written as an Edge. Facts are
the steady-state ingestion path.
_Avoid_: event (bare), message

**Bulk load**:
The onboarding door: millions of edges for one workspace in one burst,
loaded directly, built for the day an attorney brings their whole
practice.
_Avoid_: import (intake-api owns that word), migration

**Neighborhood**:
The answer to "what relates to this?" — edges around one reference, or
around everything tied to one party, always within one workspace.
_Avoid_: graph (bare), traversal (the mechanism, not the answer)

**Case scoping**:
The second scoping field every Edge carries, beside the workspace: which
case the relationship belongs to. Like the workspace id, it is a shared
platform identifier, not another service's data — it exists so "everything
related within this case" is one indexed read, never a cross-service join.
_Avoid_: matter, dispute

**Workspace scoping**:
The property the whole design rests on: no query ever crosses a
workspace. Every edge belongs to exactly one workspace; every read is
filtered by it first. Each workspace's graph stays small forever, and
the platform scales by adding capacity per workspace group.
_Avoid_: tenant, org
