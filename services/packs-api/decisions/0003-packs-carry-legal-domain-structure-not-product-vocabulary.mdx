Ruled 2026-08-31 (Christopher), correcting the pack model shipped in the
FS-894 wave-1 build. Supersedes the placeholder-flag comments in
`seed/family-law.json`'s v1 `_comment` field and in ADR 0002's mention of
`finding_types`/`document_categories` as example section kinds. Full
research trail: `scratch_pads/pack-seam-research.md`,
`scratch_pads/pack-seam-architecture.md`,
`scratch_pads/family-law-taxonomy-draft.md`.

## The correction

Christopher rejected the shipped pack framing directly: "family law pack
should be legal specific, right? So all of the laws related to family
law in every state?" and, twice, "doesn't seem like the right seam"
about finding types and document categories living in a pack.

A pack IS: the legal-domain structure of one practice area — its
lifecycle phases (unchanged, ruled by DESIGN.md section 6), its areas of
practice, the causes of action those areas produce, and the
order/provision types a case in that practice area recognizes. This is
the shape a family-law attorney would recognize regardless of which
state they practice in.

A pack is NOT: per-jurisdiction statute text or process instances (that
is `state-law-api`'s job, already modeled as `(jurisdiction,
practice_area)` — ADR 0004 in that service), or a product service's own
detection/classification vocabulary (`finding_type` is analysis-api's;
`document_category`/`DocumentType` is intake-api's/files-api's).

## What changed

- `seed/family-law.json` republishes as a new, immutable pack version
  (ADR 0001 - v1's `finding_types`/`document_categories` sections stay
  exactly as originally published and readable forever; this is a new
  version, not an edit). The new version drops `finding_types` and
  `document_categories` and adds `legal_domain`: `areas_of_practice`,
  `causes_of_action` (each pointing at an area by key), and
  `order_provision_types` (reusing the mined provision-behavior
  vocabulary from `analysis-api/engine.py`, per
  `family-law-taxonomy-draft.md`).
- A new `PracticeArea` table is the registry every pack publish now
  validates `practice_area_key` against (`store.publish` raises
  `UnknownPracticeAreaError`, surfaced as `422`). This replaces the old
  `SELECT DISTINCT practice_area_key FROM pack` as the source of truth
  for "what practice areas exist" (ADR 0002 previously treated the Pack
  table itself as that source; a registry that other services can
  register against and validate against did not exist before this).
  `GET /internal/practice-areas/{key}` and `POST
  /internal/practice-areas/{key}` (register, idempotent) are the new
  doors. `GET /internal/practice-areas` (list) now reads the registry,
  not the Pack table.
- The `legal_domain` section gets a documented, publish-time-validated
  wire contract (`_validate_legal_domain_section` in `store.py`) —
  `sections` itself stays untyped JSON (ADR 0002 unchanged), but this one
  named section kind is shape-checked at the door, the same discipline
  state-law-api already uses for its own sections (ADR 0004 there).

## What did not change

- `Pack.sections` stays a JSON blob keyed by section kind — ADR 0002's
  "extensible data, not enums" argument still holds. Adding
  `legal_domain` as a new key required zero schema migration to `Pack`.
- Lifecycle phases: unchanged content, unchanged section key,
  unchanged consumer (`case-api`'s `PacksReader.validate_phase`).
- `finding_type`/`document_category` vocabularies do not move INTO this
  service either — they stay owned by analysis-api and
  intake-api/files-api respectively. A pack does not define them; it may
  reference them by key in a future section, but does not do so yet.

## Why not delete the sections silently

The deletion test in `pack-seam-research.md` Part 2 confirmed zero live
consumers of `finding_types`/`document_categories` from packs-api today
(grep-confirmed against analysis-api and intake-api). Removing them from
the published pack changes nothing observable for any existing caller —
proof this correction is safe to land without a consumer-side migration.
