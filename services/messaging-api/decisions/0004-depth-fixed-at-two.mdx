Date: 2026-08-30

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Deeper traversal gathers more context but produces review screens no
client can honestly read before sending. Christopher ruled: two levels,
fixed.

## Decision

Depth 1 is the structural ring: records structurally connected to the
root, the root's attachment, and the root's notes. Depth 2 is the
analytical ring from analysis-api: findings referencing gathered records,
and their pattern groups. The analytical hop always runs; the cap is
hard-coded, never a client knob.

## Consequences

The review gate stays readable, traversal stays deterministic and cheap,
and the attorney UI's level-1/level-2 grouping is this same number rather
than a separate concept.

## Amendment (2026-08-30): depth 1 discovered via relations, hydrated from evidence

Depth 1's structural ring is no longer read straight off evidence-api's
thread lookup. It is discovered by asking relations-api which records are
connected to the root, then hydrated one at a time from evidence-api;
node ids are assigned in discovery order over that ordered relations
answer, so the walk stays deterministic. The root's attachment and notes
are unchanged — they never came from the thread lookup.

Christopher ruled snapshot-now: the walk waits for nothing and reads
whatever the relations map knows at that moment. If relations-api is
ahead of evidence-api (an edge exists but the record hasn't landed yet)
or behind it (evidence has moved past what an edge still says), that
single discovered record is silently dropped from this snapshot rather
than the walk stalling or failing. The review gate is where a client
finds out their context was still settling — not the backend.
