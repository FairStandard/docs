> Written when this repo was `package-api`. The service is

Date: 2026-08-30

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The Case Package feature (litigant sends context to attorney as one
trustworthy bundle) needs traversal across evidence-api and analysis-api,
its own workflow state (draft → reviewed → finalized), and an attorney
read surface. Evidence-api was deliberately built as an insert-only leaf
with no outbound calls; case-api is scoped to cases, orders, and legal
rules. Christopher ruled 2026-08-30 against folding packaging into
either.

## Decision

Packages live in their own service, package-api: its own Postgres, its
own traversal and review-gate logic, its own vault API. It READS
evidence-api and analysis-api over their APIs only, and WRITES exactly
one thing back: an audit entry into evidence-api's existing chain when a
Package is delivered. Client notes also live here — no other service owns
free-text annotations, and notes exist to travel inside Packages.

## Consequences

One more repo and database to run, in exchange for the endless-scaling
property: packaging grows along its own axis (new node types, new
traversal rules) without dragging evidence-api's or case-api's schemas
along. Evidence-api stays a leaf.

## Amendment (2026-08-30): relations-api as the third upstream

The walk's depth-1 discovery moves off evidence-api's thread lookup and
onto relations-api: package-api asks the relations map who is
structurally connected to the root record, then hydrates each answer
from evidence-api. Packages now READS three services — evidence-api,
analysis-api, relations-api — over their APIs only; the one write-back
(the audit entry on delivery) is unchanged. This is snapshot-now, by
ruling: the walk reads whatever the relations map holds at that instant
and waits for nothing to settle. Evidence-api stays a leaf; relations-api
stays read-only from here.
