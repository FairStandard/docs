Date: 2026-08-30

## Status

Superseded by [0006](/services/messaging-api/decisions/0006-identity-swap-real-verify-and-registry) —
identity-api now exists and serves both the caller's case relationship
and the relationship vocabulary as real data. See that ADR for what
changed and why.

## Context

Litigant and attorney roles, and who belongs to which case, will be owned
by identity-api — which is not carved yet. Blocking this service on that
carve would stall the feature; Christopher ruled: build now with a
stand-in.

## Decision

Every route is workspace-scoped by X-Workspace-Id and reads the caller's
CASE RELATIONSHIP from an X-Case-Relationship header — the four-axis
audience value the platform canon stores on the case-team record
(personas never gate capability access; what this service checks is flow
STRUCTURE: the parties to a case send context, their representing
professional receives it). The relationship vocabulary is an extensible
registry seeded with the canon's values, never a closed enum. Nobody
reads excluded content. Identity-api replaces the header's SOURCE later —
the checks themselves do not move.

## Consequences

The relationship checks are real and tested from day one — including
full parity for self-represented parties — and swapping in identity-api
changes where the relationship comes from, not what the service permits.
