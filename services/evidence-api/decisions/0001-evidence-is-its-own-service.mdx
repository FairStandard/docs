Ruled 2026-08-30 under the endless-scaling standard: evidence — records,
files, and the audit chain — gets a dedicated store rather than living
inside the case service (would need prying out as it grows) or inside
intake (would weld the upload flow's scaling to the archive's). Intake
writes evidence once over this API; every other service reads it here. The
door never moves again.
