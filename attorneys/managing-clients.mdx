Every client case you are connected to in Vero lives in one place — your attorney dashboard. Instead of chasing documents across email threads, tracking deadlines in a separate calendar, and repeating intake questions at every meeting, you have a single view of each client's evidence vault, the assembled law for their case, and everything that needs to happen next. When clients upload something new or a deadline shifts, you see it. When you need to send a message or review a filing, it's in the same workspace.

## Your attorney dashboard

Your dashboard is the first screen you see when you log in. It shows every case you are currently connected to, with flagged deadlines surfaced across all of them in chronological order so the most urgent items never get buried. Unread messages from clients appear in the notifications panel, and a recent activity feed highlights evidence uploaded in the last 48 hours across all your active cases. You never have to navigate into each individual case just to find out whether anything changed.

## Connecting to a client case

<Steps>
  <Step title="Client Creates the Case">
    Your client creates a case in Vero and selects the appropriate jurisdiction and case type. The engine begins assembling the applicable law immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client Invites You">
    The client enters your email address from within the case workspace. You receive an email invitation tied to that specific case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept and Connect">
    Click the link in the invitation email and accept the connection. The case appears in your dashboard immediately — no additional setup required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Existing Work">
    Any evidence the client uploaded before inviting you, the assembled law for the jurisdiction and case type, and the full case history are immediately available. You start informed, not from scratch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you see in a client case

Once you are connected to a case, you have access to the full case workspace:

- Full evidence vault with all uploaded documents, recordings, and financials
- Assembled law for the case's jurisdiction and type
- Timeline of all case events
- Upcoming deadlines with the governing procedural rules attached
- Next Steps recommendations generated from the facts and applicable law
- Complete message history between all parties in the workspace

<Note>
  You can be connected to multiple client cases simultaneously. All of them appear in your dashboard, with deadlines sorted chronologically across all cases.
</Note>

## Managing permissions

Clients control who has access to their case. As the connected attorney, you can view all case materials and contribute — uploading documents, sending messages, and annotating the assembled law. The client remains the case owner. If you need additional permissions, such as filing on behalf of the client or modifying case settings, the client must grant those explicitly in their case settings. This keeps clients actively informed and in control of their own matter, which improves engagement and reduces disputes about case direction.

<Tip>
  Ask your clients to upload documents before your first meeting. By the time you connect to the case, the engine will have already analyzed the material — saving you hours of intake work.
</Tip>
