No attorney holds all the law for every case type in every jurisdiction — the statutory codes alone run to thousands of sections, and that's before you factor in local rules, controlling precedent, and the procedural specifics of each county courthouse. On top of that, organizing the evidence clients hand you in folders, phone dumps, and forwarded email threads quietly consumes hours that should be billable. And when firms deploy their own client portals, adoption is poor — clients don't log in because the tool isn't built around their case. Vero solves all three problems in one platform: it assembles the law automatically, it organizes evidence the moment it's uploaded, and clients actually use it because their deadlines, documents, and next steps are already inside.

## Why clients adopt it

The failure mode of most firm-provided tools is simple: clients don't have a reason to open them. A portal that holds static intake forms or occasional updates competes with email and loses. Vero is different because the client's own case lives inside it. Their deadlines are there. Their evidence is there. Their next steps — generated from the assembled law and the facts of their specific case — are there. A client who opens Vero on Monday morning isn't doing it as a favor to you; they're doing it because that's where their case is. That daily engagement means evidence is uploaded promptly, messages are answered quickly, and the record is current by the time you need it.

<Tip>
  When you connect to a client case, you immediately have access to all evidence they have already uploaded, the assembled law, and the full case history — no separate onboarding.
</Tip>
