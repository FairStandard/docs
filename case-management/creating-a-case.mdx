Every case in FairStandard starts with a few simple setup steps. Once you create a case, the law assembly engine immediately goes to work — pulling the governing statutes, court rules, and local procedural requirements for your state and case type so your workspace is ready before you upload a single document.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Dashboard">
    Log in at [app.fairstandard.com](https://app.fairstandard.com). From your dashboard, click **New Case**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name Your Case">
    Enter a descriptive name (e.g., *Smith v. Jones Custody 2026*). This label is for your reference only and does not appear on any court filing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Jurisdiction">
    Choose the state where the case is filed. FairStandard uses this selection to pull the correct statutes, court rules, and local procedural requirements for your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Case Type">
    Select from the supported case types — such as Custody, Divorce, Child Support, or Modification. The law assembly engine begins working as soon as you save your selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Attorney (Optional)">
    If you are represented, enter your attorney's email address to give them shared access to the case workspace. They will receive an invitation to join and can collaborate on the case immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can create a case without an attorney and invite one later. The assembled law, evidence vault, and all other features are available to you immediately — regardless of representation status.
</Tip>

## What Happens After You Create a Case

As soon as you save your case, the law assembly engine starts running in the background. Within moments, the **Assembled Law** section of your workspace is populated with the governing statutes, procedural rules, and any local court requirements tied to your state and case type. You do not need to search for the relevant law — it is already there, organized and ready to reference as you build your case.

From this point, you can upload evidence, track deadlines, and begin working through your next steps. Every feature of the platform is available from the moment your case is created.

<Note>
  Currently supported jurisdictions include all 50 US states for family law case types. Additional jurisdictions and case types are added regularly.
</Note>
