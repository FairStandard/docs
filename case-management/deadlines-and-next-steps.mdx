Many cases are not lost on the merits — they are lost on mechanics. A missed response deadline, a filing in the wrong format, a hearing date that slipped off the calendar. FairStandard eliminates that risk by automatically tracking every procedural deadline in your case and surfacing the specific next action you need to take, in plain language, before it is too late.

## The Deadlines Panel

The **Deadlines** panel in your case workspace gives you a complete view of every time-sensitive obligation in your case. This includes upcoming hearings, filing deadlines, response windows, and statute of limitations milestones.

Each deadline in the panel shows:

- **The date** — when the action is due
- **What is due** — a plain-language description of the filing, response, or appearance required
- **The governing rule** — the specific procedural rule or court order that creates the deadline, with a citation

Deadlines are generated automatically from your jurisdiction's rules and updated as new events are added to your case. You always see the full picture in one place.

## Next Steps

Next Steps are context-aware recommended actions generated by the engine based on the current state of your case. Rather than leaving you to determine what comes next, FairStandard tells you — with specifics.

For example, if the opposing party files a motion, your Next Steps panel might show: *You have 14 days to respond to the motion filed on Aug 12. Here is what to include.* Each recommendation is tied to the procedural rule that drives it and to the relevant documents already in your vault.

Next Steps update automatically as your case progresses, so the panel always reflects where things stand right now.

<Note>
  Deadlines are derived from your jurisdiction's procedural rules and the events in your case. Always verify critical deadlines with your attorney or the court clerk.
</Note>

## Setting Reminders

For any deadline in the panel, you can set a reminder to make sure it does not catch you off guard. Click the deadline to open its detail view, then select **Set Reminder**. You can choose to receive the reminder by email, as an in-app notification, or both — and you can set reminders at multiple intervals (for example, 14 days before and again 3 days before).

## When Deadlines Change

Court schedules change. If a court order, continuance, or new filing shifts a deadline, you can update it manually from the deadline's detail view. When you update a deadline, FairStandard recalculates any downstream dependencies — so if one date moves, the related obligations adjust automatically rather than leaving you with a cascade of stale dates to fix by hand.

<Tip>
  Check your Next Steps panel after every new filing or court order. The engine re-analyzes your case each time new documents are added, and your recommended actions will reflect the updated state of the case.
</Tip>
