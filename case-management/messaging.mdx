FairStandard includes built-in messaging so that every conversation about your case lives in the same place as your evidence, deadlines, and filings. You never have to piece together what was said across a scattered thread of emails, texts, and voicemails again.

## Why Centralized Messaging Matters

Most cases run over a mix of email, text messages, and phone calls. Important decisions get buried in inboxes, context gets lost between conversations, and clients are often left wondering where things stand. FairStandard solves this by centralizing all case communication directly inside the case record — tied to the same workspace where your documents, deadlines, and analysis live. Every message is in context, every conversation is findable, and nothing gets lost.

## Sending a Message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Case">
    Navigate to the case from your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Messages Tab">
    Click the **Messages** tab in the case workspace to open your conversation panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type Your Message">
    Click into the message field and write your message. You can use formatting to organize longer communications clearly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a File (Optional)">
    If your message relates to a specific document, click the attachment icon and select any file already in your Evidence vault. No re-uploading required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send**. Your attorney or legal service provider will be notified according to their notification preferences.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Message Threads

Messages in FairStandard are organized by topic rather than presented as a flat inbox. You can start a new thread for a specific document, deadline, motion, or issue — keeping discussions focused and easy to follow. When you need to revisit a conversation, you find it by topic rather than scrolling through an undifferentiated stream of messages.

## File Attachments

Any file already in your Evidence vault can be attached to a message directly from the message composer. Because the file is already part of your case record, attaching it to a message does not create a duplicate — it simply links the existing file to that conversation, so the recipient can view it in full context.

<Note>
  Messaging is available to both represented and self-represented litigants. If you do not have an attorney, you can use messaging to communicate with any legal service provider you connect with through the Marketplace.
</Note>

## Notifications

To control how you are notified about new messages, go to **Settings → Notifications** and update your preferences for the **Messages** category. You can choose email notifications, in-app notifications, or both. You can also set different preferences for different cases if you have more than one active case in your account.

<Tip>
  Keep all case communication inside FairStandard. Messages are stored as part of your case record and can be referenced alongside your evidence and filings — giving you a complete, unified picture of everything that has happened in the case.
</Tip>
