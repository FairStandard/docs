Your listing did what it is meant to do: someone browsing the directory matched your specialties, jurisdictions, or service kinds, read your profile, and sent a consultation request. What you do next is entirely a matter of <span className="kw">judgment</span> — Vero's part is limited to getting the request to you and keeping a record of what you decide.

## What to do

Check your queue and open the request to read the message and how to reach the person back. Decide whether it is a fit for what you handle, the same way you would weigh any inquiry that reached you outside Vero. If it is, select **Accept**; if it is not, select **Decline** — either way, your decision is final on your end, and nothing about it is reversible from that screen.

If you accept, reach out to arrange next steps: a call, an email, a meeting, whatever you would normally do after agreeing to a first conversation with someone who found you on their own.

## What Vero does for you

Vero puts the request in a queue tied to your listing, private to the people at your organization with access to it — no other provider in the directory ever sees it. It keeps a plain, permanent record of whether a request is still queued, was accepted, or was declined, so nothing gets lost between when it arrived and when you act on it.

Vero does not rank requests, does not decide for you which ones are worth your time, and does not yet connect an accepted request to a case or open a message thread on your behalf. Whether to take the engagement, and how to run it once you do, is entirely your decision to make.
