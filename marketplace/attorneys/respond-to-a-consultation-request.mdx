A consultation request sits in your queue until you decide what to do with it — nothing about it moves on its own. This walks you through reading one and making that <span className="kw">decision</span>.

## Before you begin

You need an active listing that has received at least one request. Check your queue to see what is waiting.

1. From the sidebar, select **Marketplace**, then select your listing.
2. Select **Requests** to see the queue.
3. Open a request to read the message and how to reach the person back.
4. Decide whether it is a fit for what you handle.
5. Select **Accept** or **Decline**.

## What happens next

If you accept, Vero keeps a permanent record of it: who asked, which listing, which request, and when. It does not add the person to a case, open a shared workspace, or start a message thread between you — that connection is not built yet. Reach out to them the same way you would after any other consultation you found on your own.

If you decline, the request stays visible in your queue as declined. Nothing else happens on your end, and the person is free to send a request to a different listing.
