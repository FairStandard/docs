A consultation request is an ask to talk to you — not a booked appointment and not a case that has been assigned to you. Understanding the three states it can be in explains what to expect once one <span className="kw">arrives</span> in your queue.

## A request starts queued

When someone sends a request to your listing, it is marked queued and placed in the queue tied to that listing. It stays there until you act on it — nothing about browsing your queue or the passage of time moves it out of that state on its own. No case, account, or engagement is required on the requester's side to send one; the only information attached is their message and a way to reach them back.

## Accepting or declining is your call, and only yours

Nothing in the Marketplace decides for you. You review the request and either accept or decline it. There is no automatic acceptance, no scoring, and no ranking of requests ahead of others — every request in your queue is treated the same until you look at it. A declined request stays visible as declined; it is not deleted or hidden, and declining it does not stop that person from reaching out to a different listing.

## What accepting a request does today

Vero keeps a permanent record that the request was accepted: who asked, which listing, which request, and when. That record does not change or disappear later, regardless of what happens next.

What does not happen automatically, at least not yet, is any connection between you and that person's case. Accepting a request does not add you to a case, open a shared workspace, or start a message thread for the two of you — those connections are not built yet. An accepted request means you agreed to talk to them; everything after that, including how you actually work together, happens outside the Marketplace for now, the same as arranging a first conversation with anyone else who found you on their own.

## A request is not a booked meeting

Nothing about the queue holds a calendar slot or schedules a call. If an actual appointment time needs setting, that gets worked out with the person directly once you have accepted their request — the Marketplace only tracks whether you agreed to talk, not when.
