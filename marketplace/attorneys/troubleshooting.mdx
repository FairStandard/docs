Most questions here come down to a listing that has not gone live yet, or a queue that is not scoped the way you expected. The list below covers what attorneys and firm staff run into <span className="kw">most</span>.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My listing won't activate">
    Activation checks whether your organization has an active Marketplace subscription on file. If it does not, activation is refused and your listing stays a draft — that is a billing question for whoever manages your organization's account, not a problem with the listing content itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My listing is active but nobody is finding it">
    Filtering matches an exact category, not the words inside your biography. Check that you have actually published the specific specialties, jurisdictions, and service kinds you expect someone to filter by — a broad biography does not make you findable if the checkboxes underneath it are narrow or missing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I haven't received any consultation requests">
    Confirm your listing is active, not still a draft. If it is active, the specialties, jurisdictions, or service kinds you published may be narrower than what people are searching for — an empty queue reflects who has found you so far, not a fault in how requests are delivered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I accepted a request but don't see the person connected to a case">
    That is expected today. Accepting a request records that it happened, but it does not add the person to a case or open a message thread automatically — that connection is not built yet. Arrange next steps with them directly, the same way you would after any other consultation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A colleague sees requests I thought were only mine">
    Your queue is scoped to your listing, not to you personally. Anyone at your organization with access to Marketplace tools for that listing sees the same requests you do — there is no separate view for "requests sent to me" specifically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I declined a request by mistake">
    A declined request stays visible as declined and cannot be reopened from that state. Reach out to the person directly using the contact information on their original request if you want to continue the conversation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck

If none of the above accounts for what you're seeing, contact [support@fairstandard.com](mailto:support@fairstandard.com) with your listing name and a description of what you expected to see.
