Getting your listing live takes a few minutes once you know the shape of it: build the profile, activate it, then check back for requests. This guide walks you through <span className="kw">building</span> your listing and responding to your first consultation request.

## What you'll learn

- How to create your Marketplace listing
- How to build your profile: display name, headline, biography, specialties, jurisdictions, and service kinds
- How to activate your listing so it shows up in the public directory
- How to check your consultation queue for new requests
- How to accept or decline a request

## Before you begin

You need access to your organization's workspace in Vero. You do not need an active subscription to build and save a listing as a draft — you only need one to activate it. If you are not sure whether your organization has an active Marketplace subscription, check with whoever manages your organization's account before you try to go live.

### Step 1: Create your listing

From the sidebar, select **Marketplace**, then select **New listing**.

### Step 2: Build your profile

Set your display name, headline, and biography — this is what a prospective client reads on your public profile. Choose the specialties, jurisdictions, and service kinds that apply to you. These come from Vero's published list, and they are what someone filtering the directory has to match to find you.

### Step 3: Save your listing

Save what you have entered. Your listing starts as a draft — nothing on it is visible in the directory yet, so you can keep editing it until it is ready.

### Step 4: Activate your listing

When you are ready to go live, select **Activate**. Vero checks whether your organization has an active Marketplace subscription. If it does, your listing's status changes to active and it becomes visible to anyone browsing the directory who matches what you have published. If it does not, activation is refused and your listing stays a draft.

### Step 5: Check your queue

From your listing, select **Requests** to see any consultation requests that have come in. An empty queue means nobody has reached out yet, not that anything is wrong.

### Step 6: Accept or decline a request

Open a request to read the message and how to reach the person back, then select **Accept** or **Decline**.

## Tips

- If activation is refused, that is a billing question for your organization, not something wrong with the listing itself.
- Filtering matches an exact category, not the words inside your biography — publish the specific specialties, jurisdictions, and service kinds you actually want to be found for.
- Your queue is scoped to your listing, not to you personally. If more than one person at your organization has access to it, everyone sees the same requests.
- Declining a request does not delete it or hide it from your queue — it stays visible as declined.
