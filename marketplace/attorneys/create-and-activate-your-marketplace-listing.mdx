A listing has two states that matter: a draft you can edit freely, and active, which is the only state anyone browsing the directory can see. This walks you through <span className="kw">building</span> one and taking it live.

## Before you begin

You need access to your organization's workspace in Vero. You do not need an active subscription to build and save a listing — only to activate it. If you are not sure whether your organization has one, check with whoever manages your organization's account before step 5.

1. From the sidebar, select **Marketplace**, then select **New listing**.
2. Set your display name, headline, and biography.
3. Choose the specialties, jurisdictions, and service kinds that apply to you, from Vero's published list.
4. Save the listing.
5. When you are ready to go live, select **Activate**.

## What happens next

Saving a listing without activating it keeps it as a draft — you can keep editing the profile, and nothing on it is visible in the directory yet. Selecting **Activate** checks whether your organization has an active Marketplace subscription on file. If it does, your listing's status changes to active and it becomes visible to anyone browsing the directory who matches the specialties, jurisdictions, or service kinds you published. If it does not, activation is refused and your listing stays a draft — that is a billing question for your organization to resolve, not a problem with what you entered.

You can deactivate an active listing at any time to stop appearing in the directory. A deactivated listing keeps its profile content, so reactivating it later does not mean starting over.
