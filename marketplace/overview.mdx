The FairStandard Marketplace is built into your case from day one. Whether you need a licensed attorney to take the wheel, a notary to certify a sworn statement, a custody evaluator to submit a professional assessment, or a mediator to help you reach agreement before a final hearing, you can find and connect with them here — without leaving your case.

## The Problem the Marketplace Solves

Finding legal help today means running a Google search, asking a friend, or cold-calling offices until someone picks up. For a self-represented litigant who realizes mid-case that they need professional help, there is no clear path from *I need help* to *someone took my case*. The Marketplace is that path. Every provider listed has a profile on the platform, accepts consultation requests through the platform, and can be connected directly to your existing case with a single click — so nothing gets lost in the handoff.

## How the Marketplace Works

Search by jurisdiction, case type, and provider role to see professionals who match your situation. Browse profiles to review credentials, practice areas, languages spoken, and fee structures. When you find someone you want to talk to, request a consultation directly from their profile — they will receive your request and respond through the platform. Once you engage a provider, connect them to your case with one click. From that moment on, they have access to the shared case workspace and can get to work immediately.

<Tip>
  You can search the Marketplace at any point in your case — whether you are just starting out or already deep into litigation and need specialized help.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Connecting a provider to your case gives them access to the shared case workspace. You control which cases they can see.
</Note>
