Once you've found a listing worth talking to, reaching out takes one message and one form. This walks you through sending a <span className="kw">request</span> and knowing what to expect once it's in.

## Before you begin

You need a listing open — see the guide on finding one if you haven't picked a listing yet. You don't need a case or an account to send a request.

1. Open the listing you want to contact.
2. Select **Request a consultation**.
3. Enter your name or another way for the provider to know who's asking.
4. Write a short message describing what you need.
5. Submit the request.

## What happens next

Your request is added to that provider's queue, marked as queued, and stays private between you and them. The provider decides whether to accept or decline it — there's no automatic acceptance and no time limit shown for how long a decision takes.

If the request is accepted, Vero records that it happened, but it doesn't yet connect the provider to your case or open a message thread for the two of you. Arrange whatever comes next — a call, an email, a meeting — with the provider directly, the same way you would after any other consultation. If the request is declined, nothing else happens on your end; you're free to send a request to a different listing.
