The Marketplace directory is open to browse any time, whether or not you have a case. This walks you through <span className="kw">narrowing</span> it down to the right person for what you need.

## Before you begin

You don't need anything set up to browse — every active listing is visible to anyone.

1. From the navigation, select **Marketplace**.
2. Filter by specialty (for example, child custody or mediation) to narrow the list to providers who cover it.
3. Filter by jurisdiction if you only want providers who list the county or state your case is in.
4. Filter by service type — a consultation, full representation, document review, limited-scope representation, or mediation services — if you already know what kind of help you're after.
5. Select a listing to open the provider's full profile.

## What happens next

The listing opens to the provider's profile: their display name, headline, biography, and the specialties, jurisdictions, and service types they've published. Nothing on the profile shows their billing or business setup — you're only seeing what they chose to publish about themselves.

Filtering matches an exact category, not the words inside a biography, so a search with no results usually means the category was too narrow rather than that nobody's listed. Try a broader specialty, drop the jurisdiction filter, or try a related service type before assuming nobody fits.
