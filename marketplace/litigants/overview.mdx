The Marketplace is a public <span className="kw">directory</span> of attorneys and legal service providers — you can browse it without a case or an account. This page explains what a listing shows, how filtering it works, and what actually happens once you send a consultation request.

## How it works

A listing is one provider's public presence in the Marketplace: a display name, a headline, a short biography, and the specialties, jurisdictions, and service types they've chosen to publish. A listing does not show anything about the provider's billing or business setup — only what they put on their own profile.

Filtering the directory works by exact match, not by searching the words inside a biography. You narrow the list by specialty (family law, divorce, child custody, child support, adoption, domestic violence, or mediation, today), by jurisdiction, and by service type (a consultation, full representation, document review, limited-scope representation, or mediation services). An empty result usually means the category was too narrow, not that nobody's listed — try a broader specialty or drop the jurisdiction filter before assuming there's no match.

When you find someone worth talking to, you send a consultation request straight from their listing, with a short message about what you need. That request goes into a queue only that provider can see. They decide whether to accept or decline it — nothing in the Marketplace decides it for them, and no other provider ever sees your request.

## What you can do here

- Browse the directory of attorneys and legal service providers without a case or an account.
- Filter listings by specialty, jurisdiction, and service type.
- Open a listing to read a provider's profile.
- Send a consultation request directly from a listing.
- Check whether a request you sent is still waiting, accepted, or declined.

## Who sees what

Anyone can browse the directory, whether or not they have a case in Vero — it doesn't ask who you are until you send a request. A consultation request you send is private between you and the one provider you sent it to; nobody else, including other providers in the directory, can see it.

## Availability

Only active listings show up in the directory. A provider who hasn't published a listing, or whose listing isn't currently active, won't appear no matter how you filter. Today, the specialties and service types in the directory are all family law; more categories are added over time as an addition, never a change to the ones already there.

Accepting your request is a real decision the provider makes, and Vero keeps a record that it happened. What Vero doesn't yet do automatically is connect that provider to your case or open a message thread between you — that part isn't built yet, so plan on arranging next steps with them directly, the same way you would after any other consultation.
