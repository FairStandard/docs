Legal cases often require more than an attorney. A notary certifies the affidavit you are about to file. A custody evaluator submits a professional assessment the court will rely on. A mediator helps you and the other party reach agreement before a final hearing. The Vero Marketplace lists all of these specialists in one place, searchable by type and jurisdiction, so you can find who you need and connect them to your case without starting from scratch.

## Types of legal service providers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Notaries">
    Notaries certify signatures on legal documents. They are commonly required for affidavits, sworn statements, and other documents filed with the court. Finding a notary through the Marketplace means they can receive and return documents through the platform.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Custody Evaluators">
    Custody evaluators are licensed mental health professionals who assess parenting capacity and make formal recommendations to the court in contested custody cases. Their reports carry significant weight in custody determinations, and many judges give them substantial deference.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mediators">
    Mediators are neutral third parties who help both sides reach an agreement outside of court. Many jurisdictions require mediation before a final hearing. A successful mediation can resolve your case faster and at lower cost than continued litigation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Process Servers">
    Process servers handle the formal service of process when you need to serve legal documents on another party. Proper service is a legal requirement, and using a professional ensures it is done correctly and documented.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Finding the right provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Marketplace and Filter">
    Open the Marketplace and filter by **Provider Type** and **Jurisdiction** to see specialists who serve your area and match your need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Profiles">
    Review provider profiles including credentials, availability, and fee structures to find the right fit for your case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a Consultation or Booking">
    Request a consultation or booking directly through their profile. The provider will receive your request and respond through the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to Your Case">
    Connect the provider to your case if ongoing access is needed. They will have immediate access to the relevant documents and workspace without any manual handoff.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Mediators are required before the final hearing in many states. Check your Next Steps panel — Vero will flag this requirement when it applies to your case.
</Note>
