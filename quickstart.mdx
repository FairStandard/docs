---

Getting up and running with FairStandard takes only a few minutes. By the end of this guide you'll have an account, an active case with your jurisdiction's law assembled, and your first documents processed by the evidence engine — ready to start surfacing what matters.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Go to [app.fairstandard.com](https://app.fairstandard.com) and click **Get Started**. Choose your role — select **Litigant** if you're a party in a case (with or without an attorney), or **Attorney / Provider** if you're a legal professional. Fill in your name, email address, and a secure password, then submit the form. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a New Case">
    From your dashboard, click **New Case**. Enter a case name that's meaningful to you (for example, "Smith v. Jones — Custody"), then select your **jurisdiction** (the state where the case is filed) and your **case type** — such as Custody, Divorce, Child Support, or Property Division. Once you save, FairStandard automatically assembles the statutes, procedural rules, and local requirements that apply to your case, so you're working from accurate, current law from the start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Your Documents">
    Open the **Evidence** tab inside your case and drag your files directly into the vault, or click **Upload Files** to browse. You can upload in bulk — there's no need to sort or label anything before you drop it in. Supported formats include PDF, DOCX, MP3, MP4, PNG, JPG, and common message exports (such as SMS backups and email threads). Court orders, financial statements, opposing filings, voicemails, text screenshots — bring everything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the Engine Work">
    After upload, FairStandard processes your documents automatically. The engine organizes your evidence into a structured timeline, then identifies what matters: patterns of behavior across communications, financial discrepancies and potential hidden assets, unfulfilled discovery requests, and violations of existing court orders. Every finding is cited back to the source document so you can verify it and use it directly. You don't need to tag files or tell the engine what to look for — it's built to find signal in large, messy document sets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Your Case Workspace">
    Open your **Case Workspace** to see everything in one view: the assembled law for your jurisdiction, your full evidence timeline, upcoming deadlines, and the next recommended steps for your case. If you're represented, use the **Messages** tab to communicate with your attorney directly inside the platform — every message is tied to your case record. Review the engine's findings, check your next steps, and use the Learning Center if you want deeper context on any part of the process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Upload everything you have, even if you're unsure it's relevant. The engine is designed to find signal in large, messy document sets.
</Tip>
